Cost To Install Impact Windows In Davie 2026

Updated May 24, 2026 9 min read
Written by Marco Reyes, Florida Home Improvement Specialist

How Much Does It Cost to Install Impact Windows in Davie? (2026 Guide)

Quick Answer: Installing impact windows in Davie costs between $15,000 and $50,000 for most single-family homes, with the average homeowner paying around $28,000. The biggest factors driving your price are window count, frame material, and Broward County’s strict wind load building requirements.

South Florida homes face unique wind risks every year. While Davie is inland, the entire county follows rigorous Florida Building Code standards for window strength. This ensures your home stays sealed during high winds and keeps insurance premiums manageable in a volatile market.

These prices reflect Davie market rates as of early 2026 and include materials, labor, disposal, standard permits, and cleanup. They do not include structural repairs if rot is found during removal.

What Affects the Cost in Davie

1. Window Count

Window replacement is priced per unit. A typical Davie home has between 15 and 30 windows. At $600–$1,200 per window installed depending on material, a 15-window home runs roughly $12,000–$18,000 while a 30-window home pushes $25,000–$40,000.

2. Frame Material Choice

This is the single biggest price lever you control. Vinyl frames are the most common option at $400–$700 per sq ft installed. Aluminum frames — popular in South Florida — jump to $600–$900. Wood frames are the premium choice at $800–$1,200 per sq ft but require more maintenance.

3. Labor Rates in Davie

Window installation labor in Broward County runs $45–$70 per hour, roughly 15–20% above the national average. Demand stays high year-round from storm damage repairs and insurance-mandated upgrades. After a named storm, rates can spike another 10–20%.

4. Permits and Inspections

Broward County requires a building permit for any window replacement involving structural changes. Permit fees run $150–$400 depending on project value. The process includes a final inspection. Your contractor should pull the permit — if they suggest skipping it, walk away.

5. Window Complexity

A standard rectangular window is straightforward to install. Add arches, circles, or custom shapes and labor climbs. Complex windows can add $3,000–$8,000 to the project. Sliding patio doors — common in Florida homes — use different glass at $800–$1,500 per unit.

6. Wind Load Requirements

Davie falls under Broward County wind codes. Every window must hold a Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA. This limits material options and adds $2,000–$5,000 compared to non-rated areas. Glass lamination is stricter too — thicker interlayers than standard code.

Sliding doors (TPO or modified bitumen) are specific to patio openings common in Davie homes. Different product category entirely from standard casement windows.

Florida-Specific Considerations

Building Codes: Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) governs all window replacements in Davie. Key requirements include secondary water barrier at all penetrations, specific anchor patterns for frame attachment, and enhanced flashing at all corners. These go beyond what most states require.

Hurricane Zone: Davie is in Broward County, which follows strict wind load maps. All window products must hold a Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA. Products are tested with missile impact tests and cyclic pressure tests that simulate hurricane conditions. Your contractor should provide NOA numbers for every product they plan to use.

Permits: Apply through Broward County’s ePlan online portal or at the Davie Building Department. Turnaround is 5–10 business days for a standard residential permit. Budget $150–$400 for the fee. Two inspections required: pre-cover and final.

Insurance Impact: This is the big one. New impact windows reset your wind mitigation profile. Many Florida insurers offer discounts of 20–45% on your premium for impact-rated glazing. This often pays for the windows over 5–7 years.

HOA: Many Davie communities restrict window frame color and style. Aluminum is often required in deed-restricted communities for uniformity. Get written HOA approval before signing a contractor agreement — changing materials mid-job is extremely expensive.

Season: Best time to install windows here is December through April — dry season, lower humidity, fewer storm damage backlogs. Avoid peak hurricane season (August–October). Rainy season (May–October) brings daily afternoon thunderstorms that delay projects.

My Safe Florida Home: If your home was built before 2008 and is homesteaded, you may qualify for a free wind inspection and matching grants up to $10,000 toward hurricane hardening improvements including window upgrades.

How to Save Money on Impact Window Installation in Davie

  1. Get at least 4 written quotes. Contractor pricing varies by 20–35% for identical scope in Davie. On a $28,000 job, that’s $5,500–$9,000 in potential savings. Make sure each quote itemizes materials, labor, tear-off, permits, and disposal separately.

  2. Schedule in dry season. December through April is slower for window installers. You’ll have more leverage to negotiate and fewer weather delays. Some contractors offer 5–10% off-season discounts.

  3. Choose vinyl over aluminum if your HOA allows it. The savings between vinyl ($400–$700/sq ft) and aluminum ($600–$900/sq ft) can be $3,000–$8,000 on a typical home.

  4. Bundle window upgrades with the roof. Adding hurricane straps or upgraded underlayment during a replacement is 40–60% cheaper than standalone projects. The combined wind mitigation improvements can also cut your insurance premium by hundreds per year.

  5. Check for My Safe Florida Home grants. If you qualify, this program pays for part of the inspection and installation. File the application before signing a contractor agreement. Be cautious of contractors who offer to “handle the insurance” — work with the program directly.

When to Install Impact Windows — Warning Signs

Your windows are 15–20 years old. Standard vinyl windows in South Florida degrade faster than in cooler climates due to intense UV, heat cycling, and salt air. A 20-year-old window in Davie has taken more abuse than a 30-year-old window in the Midwest.

Your insurance company is pressuring you. A non-renewal notice or letter requesting a window inspection means the clock is ticking. Many Florida insurers are actively dropping homes with aging single-pane glass.

Visible frame rot or warping. Check your trim after heavy rain. Water stains on interior walls mean the window seal has failed. Drafts near the frame mean insulation has broken down.

Interior noise or temperature issues. If you hear highway noise or feel heat spikes, your glass is failing. In Davie’s humidity, poor seals lead to mold fast. Multiple leaks usually mean the whole system has failed.

How to Hire a Window Contractor in Davie

  1. Verify their Florida license at MyFloridaLicense.com. You need a Certified General Contractor (CGC), Certified Roofing Contractor (CCC), or Licensed Building Contractor (LBC).

  2. Confirm active insurance. Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum $1 million) and workers comp. Call the insurer directly to verify.

  3. Check for a Broward County local business tax receipt. This confirms they’re registered locally, not a storm chaser from out of state.

  4. Get 3–4 written estimates with line-item breakdowns — material brand, glass type, number of windows, tear-off, permits, labor, timeline, and warranty.

  5. Check reviews on Google and BBB. Look for patterns in complaints, not just star ratings.

  6. Never pay more than 10% upfront. Florida Statute 489.126 restricts contractor deposits. Payment should tie to milestones: deposit, tear-off complete, mid-project, final inspection passed.

  7. Get everything in writing. Contract must include: scope, material specs with NOA numbers, dates, payment schedule, warranty terms, permit responsibility, and cleanup plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to install impact windows in Davie in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between $15,000 and $50,000. The average is around $28,000. Your actual cost depends on window count, material choice, complexity, and whether structural repairs are needed.

Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Broward County?

Yes, always. Apply through Broward County’s ePlan portal or at City Hall. Budget $150–$400. Two inspections required. Never let a contractor skip the permit — it can void your insurance and create title issues when you sell.

How long does window installation take in Davie?

Standard vinyl windows take 1–2 days for an average home. Custom aluminum takes 3–5 days. Rainy season can add 2–5 days of weather delays.

Does installing impact windows increase home value in Davie?

A new window system recoups 60–70% of its cost at resale. But the bigger value in Davie’s market is insurability — a home with impact windows is dramatically easier to sell because buyers can actually get insurance on it.

Can I install impact windows myself in Florida?

Florida’s homeowner exemption allows it if you own and occupy the home. But you still need permits, inspections, and must meet all wind code requirements. Insurance companies may refuse to cover a homeowner-installed system. For most people, it’s not worth the risk.

Does homeowners insurance cover window replacement?

Storm damage (wind, hail, fallen trees) is typically covered minus your deductible — often 2% of insured value for hurricane claims in Florida. Normal wear and aging is never covered.

Bottom Line

Installing impact windows in Davie runs $15,000 to $50,000 for most homes, with vinyl frames at the sweet spot of cost and performance. The most important thing to know: Broward County’s wind codes add cost but your new windows meet some of the toughest standards in the country — and it keeps you insurable in a market where carriers are dropping homes with aging glass every day.
