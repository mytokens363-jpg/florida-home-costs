Cost To Remodel Kitchen In Pembroke Pines 2026

Updated May 18, 2026 9 min read
Written by Marco Reyes, Florida Home Improvement Specialist

How Much Does It Cost to Remodel Kitchen in Pembroke Pines? (2026 Guide)

Quick Answer: A full kitchen remodel in Pembroke Pines costs between $15,000 and $80,000 for most homes, with the average homeowner paying around $35,000. The biggest factors driving your price are the scope of work, cabinet choices, and Broward County permit fees.

Pembroke Pines homes vary widely in age, from 1980s ranches to newer construction. Your existing layout and the condition of your plumbing lines will dictate whether you can keep the room mostly the same or need a full gut job.

Average Cost Breakdown

Cost Level Price Range Typical Scope
Budget $15,000 – $25,000 Cabinet refacing, paint, new counters
Mid-Range $25,000 – $45,000 Full cabinets, appliances, flooring
High-End $45,000 – $80,000+ Layout change, luxury materials, plumbing moves

Budget projects focus on cosmetic updates. You keep existing cabinets, refacing or painting them instead. You replace countertops with laminate or budget quartz. Lighting gets updated but plumbing stays put. This works well for 1980s homes where the layout still functions.

Mid-range is the most common choice for Pembroke Pines homeowners. You get new semi-custom cabinets, solid surface or stone countertops, and a new dishwasher or range. Flooring changes from tile to luxury vinyl or ceramic. This scope usually includes Broward County permits for electrical and plumbing.

High-end covers major structural changes. You move walls, relocate the sink, or expand the footprint. Materials include custom wood cabinets, natural stone like marble, and high-end appliances. This often requires structural engineering reviews and deeper permit scrutiny in Broward.

These prices reflect Pembroke Pines market rates as of early 2026. They include materials, labor, standard permits, and cleanup. They do not include major structural repairs if damage is found behind walls during demolition.

What Affects the Cost in Pembroke Pines

1. Kitchen Size

Pembroke Pines homes typically feature kitchens between 100 and 250 square feet. A standard 150 sq ft kitchen is the baseline for mid-range pricing. If your kitchen is larger, like in a newer estate home, expect labor hours to climb.

2. Cabinet Selection

Cabinets usually consume 40–50% of your total budget. Stock cabinets run $100–$200 per linear foot. Semi-custom options sit at $300–$600 per linear foot. Custom woodwork hits $800–$1,500 per linear foot. This is where you see the biggest price variation.

3. Labor Rates in Broward County

Skilled trades in South Florida command premium wages. Electricians charge $80–$120 per hour. Plumbers run $90–$130 per hour. Cabinet installers average $50–$75 per hour. These rates are 10–20% higher than the national average due to high demand in the Fort Lauderdale metro area.

4. Permits and Inspections

Broward County requires permits for most kitchen work involving plumbing, electrical, or structural changes. Permit fees typically run $500–$1,500 depending on the valuation of the work. The process includes a pre-inspection and final inspection. Your contractor should pull the permit.

5. Layout Changes

Moving the sink or gas line costs significantly more than keeping them in place. Relocating plumbing lines in Pembroke Pines can be tricky due to clay soil conditions. You might need to break through concrete slabs to run new pipes. This adds $3,000–$8,000 to the project.

6. HOA Restrictions

Many Pembroke Pines communities have strict Homeowner Associations. Some dictate exterior door colors or cabinet styles visible from outside. Always check your CC&Rs before ordering materials. Changing materials mid-job due to HOA rejection is extremely expensive.

Stock cabinets are the budget choice. They come in standard sizes and finishes. Good for rentals or tight budgets. You get limited customization options.

Semi-custom cabinets allow you to choose door styles and interior organizers. This is the sweet spot for most Pembroke Pines renovations. They fit better than stock but cost less than fully custom.

Quartz countertops are popular for durability. They resist heat and scratches better than natural stone. Prices vary by brand and pattern. Expect $45–$90 per square foot installed.

Granite countertops offer a unique natural look. Every slab is different. They require sealing to prevent stains. Prices range $50–$120 per square foot installed depending on the quarry.

Luxury vinyl flooring handles moisture well, which is crucial for kitchens. It mimics wood or tile at a lower cost. Best for homeowners on a budget who still want style.

Hardwood floors add value but are sensitive to water. If you choose wood, ensure it is properly sealed and maintained. It costs more upfront but lasts decades.

Florida-Specific Considerations

Building Codes: Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) governs all work in Pembroke Pines. Key requirements include GFCI outlets within 6 feet of sinks, specific electrical wiring standards, and plumbing venting. These codes protect against fires and water damage.

Flood Zones: Pembroke Pines has areas designated as flood zones. If your home is in a flood zone, your cabinet materials must be water-resistant. Use marine-grade plywood for base cabinets. This prevents rot if water lines burst.

Permits: Apply through the Broward County Building Department online portal. Turnaround is 7–14 business days for standard residential permits. Budget $500–$1,500 for fees. Two inspections required: rough-in and final.

Insurance Impact: Water damage claims are a major concern for Florida insurers. Upgrading to leak detection systems or PEX plumbing during a remodel can qualify you for discounts. A new kitchen reduces the risk of mold claims from old pipes.

HOA: Many Pembroke Pines communities restrict cabinet colors and appliance finishes. Some require matching existing community aesthetics. Get written HOA approval before ordering cabinetry.

Season: Best time to remodel is December through April. Summer heat slows down work and increases material drying times. Avoid peak hurricane season (August–October) for demolition. Rain can delay electrical and plumbing rough-ins.

My Safe Florida Home: While this program targets roofs, some grants cover flood mitigation. If your kitchen plumbing is part of a flood risk, check eligibility for mitigation upgrades.

How to Save Money on Kitchen Remodel in Pembroke Pines

  1. Keep the plumbing layout. Moving the sink or stove costs money. Keep water lines and gas lines where they are. This saves $3,000–$6,000 in labor and materials.

  2. Get at least 4 written quotes. Contractor pricing varies by 20–35% for identical scope in Pembroke Pines. On a $35,000 job, that’s $7,000–$12,000 in potential savings. Make sure each quote itemizes labor and materials separately.

  3. Choose quartz over granite. Quartz offers similar looks at a lower price point. It also requires less maintenance. You save $1,000–$3,000 on a standard 30 sq ft countertop.

  4. Refinish instead of replace cabinets. If your cabinet boxes are solid, refacing doors and drawers is cheaper. This keeps the existing structure. You save $5,000–$10,000 compared to full replacement.

  5. Do some work yourself. Demolition and painting are tasks you can do. Remove old appliances and cabinets before the crew arrives. This can reduce labor costs by 10–15% if your contractor allows it.

  6. Buy appliances during sales. Major appliances are big budget items. Wait for Memorial Day or Black Friday sales. You can save $1,000–$2,000 on a full package.

When to Remodel Your Kitchen — Warning Signs

Your cabinets are 20+ years old. Wood cabinets in Florida degrade faster due to humidity. Look for warping, loose drawers, or mold inside the boxes. Old cabinets often fail before the doors show wear.

Water damage is visible. Stains under the sink or soft spots in the floor indicate plumbing leaks. In Pembroke Pines, clay soil can shift pipes. Address leaks immediately to prevent structural rot.

Appliances are failing. If your fridge or range is over 10 years old, replacement costs are high. A new kitchen allows you to install energy-efficient models that lower your utility bills.

The layout is inefficient. A galley kitchen in a large home wastes space. If you can’t move around comfortably, a remodel improves functionality. This adds value when you sell the home.

How to Hire a Kitchen Contractor in Pembroke Pines

  1. Verify their Florida license at MyFloridaLicense.com. You need a Certified or Registered Contractor. Check for any active disciplinary actions.

  2. Confirm active insurance. Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum $1 million) and workers comp. Call the insurer directly to verify.

  3. Check for a Broward County local business tax receipt. This confirms they are registered locally, not a storm chaser from out of state.

  4. Get 3–4 written estimates with line-item breakdowns — material brand, labor hours, timeline, and warranty.

  5. Check reviews on Google and BBB. Look for patterns in complaints, not just star ratings.

  6. Never pay more than 10% upfront. Florida Statute 489.126 restricts contractor deposits. Payment should tie to milestones: deposit, rough-in complete, final inspection passed.

  7. Get everything in writing. Contract must include: scope, material specs, dates, payment schedule, warranty terms, permit responsibility, and cleanup plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Pembroke Pines in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between $15,000 and $80,000. The average is around $35,000. Your actual cost depends on layout changes, cabinet quality, and appliance selection.

Do I need a permit to remodel my kitchen in Broward County?

Yes, usually. If you change plumbing, electrical, or walls, you need a permit. Apply through Broward County Building Department. Budget $500–$1,500. Never let a contractor skip the permit — it creates title issues when you sell.

How long does a kitchen remodel take in Pembroke Pines?

Standard remodels take 6–8 weeks. Major layout changes take 10–12 weeks. Rainy season can add 2–5 days of weather delays for demolition or electrical work.

Does a kitchen remodel increase home value in Pembroke Pines?

A kitchen remodel recoups 60–70% of its cost at resale. In Pembroke Pines, updated kitchens are a major selling point because many homes are older.

Can I remodel my kitchen myself?

You can do demolition and painting. But plumbing and electrical work requires a licensed contractor in Florida. DIY work on these systems voids insurance if a leak or fire occurs.

Does homeowners insurance cover kitchen damage?

Water damage from sudden leaks is typically covered minus your deductible. Wear and tear or mold from long-term leaks is often excluded. Keep records of all work for claims.
