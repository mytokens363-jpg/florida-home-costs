# How Much Does It Cost to Replace a Water Heater in Davie? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** A standard water heater replacement in Davie costs between **$1,200 and $3,500**, with the average homeowner paying around **$2,100**. The final price depends heavily on whether you choose a traditional tank unit or a tankless system, plus the complexity of your existing plumbing and gas lines.

Davie sits in Broward County, where the plumbing code has specific requirements for seismic bracing and gas venting that differ from other parts of the state. While you won't face the same hurricane wind codes as a roof, the county's strict enforcement of gas safety standards means your permit process is non-negotiable.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$1,200 – $1,600** | 40-50 gal tank, 6ft gas line, basic permit |
| Mid-Range | **$1,600 – $2,400** | 50-75 gal tank, upgraded venting, full permit |
| High-End | **$2,400 – $3,500+** | Tankless unit, new gas line, drain pan upgrade |

**Budget** gets you a standard 40 or 50-gallon gas water heater from a major brand like Rheem or A.O. Smith. This price assumes your existing gas line is already the correct diameter and the venting is in good condition. It includes the unit, labor, and the basic Broward County permit.

**Mid-range** upgrades the unit to a 75-gallon capacity for larger families or adds a high-efficiency condensing unit. This often requires upgrading the venting pipe to Category III stainless steel and installing a new drain pan with a discharge line, which adds **$300–$600** to the bill.

**High-end** covers the switch to a tankless (on-demand) system. These units cost **$1,500–$2,500** for the equipment alone. Installation often requires a larger gas meter upgrade, a new 3/4-inch gas line, and a dedicated condensation drain, pushing the total project cost over **$3,000** in many Davie homes.

These prices reflect Davie market rates as of early 2026 and include labor, materials, disposal of the old unit, and standard permit fees. They do not include major structural repairs to the floor or walls if the new unit requires a different footprint.

## What Affects the Cost in Davie

### 1. Unit Type and Capacity

The choice between a tank and tankless is the biggest price driver. A standard 50-gallon gas tank unit costs **$600–$900** for the equipment. A high-efficiency tankless unit costs **$1,200–$2,000** for the equipment. Tankless units also require more labor to install due to the need for new gas lines and venting, often adding **$400–$800** to the labor cost compared to a simple swap.

### 2. Gas Line Requirements

Most older Davie homes have a 1/2-inch gas line. If you are installing a high-BTU tankless unit, you likely need a 3/4-inch line. Running new copper or black iron gas line from the meter to the heater adds **$300–$800** in materials and labor. If your gas meter is too small, the utility company may require an upgrade, costing an additional **$500–$1,200**.

### 3. Venting and Exhaust Systems

Broward County requires specific venting materials based on the unit's efficiency. Standard atmospheric venting uses single-wall metal pipe, which is cheap but requires a large vertical rise. High-efficiency units need stainless steel concentric venting that goes through the wall. Upgrading from old, corroded galvanized vents to new stainless steel can add **$200–$500** to the project.

### 4. Permit and Inspection Fees

Broward County requires a permit for any water heater replacement. The fee is typically calculated as **$150–$300** based on the permit value, plus a flat administrative fee. You must schedule a final inspection with the county before the job is considered complete. If the inspector finds code violations, you will pay additional fees to re-inspect.

### 5. Drain Pan and Discharge Lines

Florida Building Code requires a drain pan under any water heater located in an attic or inside a garage. If your unit is on the ground floor, a pan is still recommended. Adding a discharge pipe that runs to the outside or a floor drain costs **$150–$400**. This is critical for preventing water damage to your hardwood floors, which are common in Davie homes.

### 6. Accessibility and Location

If your water heater is in a cramped closet or a tight attic space, labor time increases. Contractors charge a premium for difficult access. Moving a unit from an attic to the garage or vice versa adds **$400–$900** due to the extra piping and structural work required.

**Standard tank** units are the most common choice for budget-conscious homeowners. They are simple to install and work with existing gas lines. They provide a steady stream of hot water but run out once the tank is empty.

**High-efficiency tank** units feature better insulation and sometimes a heat trap to reduce standby heat loss. They cost slightly more upfront but save on monthly energy bills, paying for themselves in about 5–7 years.

**Tankless gas** systems provide endless hot water and are smaller in size. They require significant upgrades to gas lines and venting in older homes. They are ideal for large families or homes with high hot water usage but have a higher upfront cost.

**Electric tank** units are cheaper to install if you already have the electrical capacity, but operating costs in Florida are higher due to electricity rates. They are often used in homes without gas service or in secondary units.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code (7th Edition, 2023) mandates seismic bracing for water heaters in certain zones, including parts of South Florida. Even if your home is not in a high-seismic zone, Davie often enforces bracing to prevent tipping during minor tremors or heavy storms. This requires a metal strapping kit costing **$50–$100**.

**Gas Safety:** Broward County strictly enforces gas leak detection and proper venting. All new gas lines must be pressure-tested and documented. If you have an older home with cast iron gas piping, the county may require a full replacement of the gas line, which can cost **$1,500–$4,000** depending on the length.

**Permits:** Apply through the Broward County Building Division online portal or at the Davie Community Center. The process takes 3–5 business days. You must have a licensed plumber pull the permit; homeowners cannot pull plumbing permits for rental properties or if they are not the owner-occupant in some cases.

**Insurance Impact:** A new water heater can lower your risk of water damage claims. While it doesn't directly lower your premium like a roof, it prevents the massive costs associated with a burst tank. Some insurers offer a **5–10%** discount on water damage coverage if you install a leak detection system with automatic shut-off valves.

**HOA:** Many Davie communities have covenants regarding the placement of utility equipment. If you are moving a water heater to the exterior of the home, you may need HOA approval. Some communities restrict the visible placement of gas meters or vent pipes.

**Season:** The peak season for water heater failures is summer when usage spikes due to showers and laundry. Booking in late spring (March–April) or late fall (October–November) often yields better availability and pricing than during the heat of summer.

**My Safe Florida Home:** While this program focuses heavily on roofs and windows, some rebates may apply to plumbing upgrades if they are part of a broader "hardening" project. Check the specific program guidelines for 2026 to see if leak detection systems qualify for rebates.

## When to Replace Your Water Heater — Warning Signs

**Your unit is 10–12 years old.** The average lifespan of a gas water heater in Florida is 10–12 years due to the hard water and high humidity. If your unit is approaching this age, plan for replacement.

**Rusty water coming from taps.** This indicates the tank is corroding from the inside. If you see rust in the hot water only, the tank is failing. Replacement is urgent to avoid a catastrophic burst.

**Water pooling around the base.** Small leaks often start at the valve or the tank seam. If you see water on the floor, the tank is likely leaking internally. Do not wait for the tank to burst; it will flood your home.

**Unusual noises.** Popping or rumbling sounds mean sediment has built up at the bottom of the tank. This reduces efficiency and heats the tank unevenly, causing stress cracks. Flushing the tank might help, but if it persists, replace the unit.

## How to Hire a Plumbing Contractor in Davie

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Search for a Certified Plumbing Contractor (CPC) or Registered Plumbing Contractor (RPC) at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). Do not hire a handyman for gas line work.

2. **Confirm active insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum **$1 million**) and workers comp. Call the insurer to verify the policy is active.

3. **Check for a Broward County local business tax receipt.** This confirms they are registered to work in Davie and are not a transient contractor.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates** with line-item breakdowns — unit model, labor hours, permit fees, gas line upgrades, and disposal.

5. **Check reviews on Google and Yelp.** Look for comments about punctuality, cleanup, and whether they pulled the correct permits.

6. **Never pay more than 10% upfront.** Florida Statute 489.126 limits deposits. Payment should be tied to milestones: deposit, unit installed, final inspection passed.

7. **Get everything in writing.** The contract must include the specific model number, warranty terms, permit responsibility, and cleanup plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a water heater replacement cost in Davie in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$1,200 and $3,500**. The average for a standard tank replacement is around **$2,100**. Tankless units cost **$2,400–$3,500** due to higher equipment and installation costs.

### Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Davie?

Yes, always. Broward County requires a permit for any gas or electric water heater replacement. The fee is typically **$150–$300**. You must schedule a final inspection with the county before the job is complete.

### How long does a water heater replacement take in Davie?

A standard tank replacement takes **2–4 hours** if no major upgrades are needed. A tankless installation or one requiring new gas lines takes **4–8 hours**. Rainy season delays are rare for indoor plumbing but can affect if exterior work is needed.

### Does a new water heater increase home value in Davie?

A new water heater does not significantly increase resale value, but it is a critical selling point. A home with a 12-year-old water heater is a red flag for buyers and appraisers.

### Can I replace my own water heater in Florida?

Florida allows homeowners to perform plumbing work on their own primary residence if they pull the permit. However, gas line work requires a licensed professional in most cases. Insurance may not cover damage from DIY gas work.

### Does homeowners insurance cover water heater replacement?

Sudden and accidental leaks are typically covered. However, gradual corrosion, rust, or wear and tear are never covered. If your tank bursts due to age, your deductible applies, but the replacement itself is an insurance claim.
