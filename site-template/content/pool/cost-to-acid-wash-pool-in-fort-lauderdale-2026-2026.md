# How Much Does It Cost to Acid Wash Pool in Fort Lauderdale? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** A professional acid wash for a standard in-ground pool in Fort Lauderdale costs between **$450 and $1,200**, with the average homeowner paying around **$750**. The price depends heavily on pool surface material, water chemistry history, and the strict safety protocols required by Broward County.

Pool surfaces in South Florida endure a brutal combination of intense UV rays, high humidity, and aggressive water balance issues. Over time, calcium deposits and algae stains turn your blue water into a chalky mess. Acid washing strips away the top layer of plaster or aggregate to reveal a fresh surface underneath.

However, this process is not a simple scrub. It involves handling highly corrosive chemicals like muriatic acid, which poses significant risks to both the pool structure and the surrounding landscape. Fort Lauderdale homeowners must navigate specific environmental regulations and safety standards that differ from other states.

## What Affects the Cost in Fort Lauderdale

### 1. Pool Size and Volume

Pool size is the primary driver of your bill. Most standard homes have pools between 15,000 and 30,000 gallons. Contractors charge based on the volume of water to drain and the surface area to treat. A **15,000-gallon** pool might cost **$450**, while a **40,000-gallon** pool can push the price toward **$1,100**.

### 2. Surface Material

The type of finish on your pool dictates the difficulty of the job. Standard white plaster is the easiest to acid wash and the most common in Fort Lauderdale. Exposed aggregate or quartz finishes are more expensive to treat because the acid can expose the aggregate unevenly if not applied perfectly.

### 3. Chemical and Disposal Fees

Muriatic acid is the standard chemical used, but it is a hazardous material. Broward County has strict rules about how this waste water must be neutralized before it enters the storm drain system. You cannot simply dump it down the drain. The cost of neutralizing agents and the labor to test the water before discharge add **$50–$150** to the total.

### 4. Accessibility and Site Conditions

If your pool is tucked behind a house with a narrow gate or surrounded by delicate landscaping, the price goes up. Contractors need to protect plants from acid splash and may need to bring in extra equipment. Heavy tree cover or limited access for a truck can add **$100–$200** to the labor cost.

### 5. Water Chemistry History

Pools in Fort Lauderdale often battle high pH and high calcium hardness due to the local water supply. If your pool has been neglected for years, the calcium buildup will be thick. This requires multiple acid applications or longer dwell times, increasing the labor hours. Neglected pools can cost **20–30% more** than regularly maintained ones.

### 6. Permit and Environmental Rules

While a simple acid wash usually doesn't require a building permit, Broward County has strict environmental codes regarding chemical discharge. Your contractor must ensure the water pH is neutralized to between **6.0 and 9.0** before releasing it. Failure to comply can result in fines. Reputable contractors factor this compliance into their pricing, ensuring you don't face legal issues.

**Medium plaster** pools are the workhorses of Fort Lauderdale. They balance size and complexity. Most homeowners fall into this bracket, paying a moderate fee for a thorough cleaning that restores the pool's brightness.

**Large plaster** pools, common in newer estates in areas like Victoria Park or Las Olas Isles, require more chemical and more time. The surface area is vast, and the neutralization process takes longer to ensure safety.

**Aggregate** finishes are popular in South Florida for their durability and aesthetic. However, they are tricky. If the acid is left on too long, it can erode the cement bond, exposing the stones too aggressively. This requires a skilled hand, driving up the cost.

**Vinyl liner** pools cannot be acid washed. The acid will eat through the liner instantly. If your pool has a vinyl liner, you must use a different cleaning method, such as a specialized brush and chlorine shock, which costs significantly less but doesn't remove deep stains.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** Florida does not have a specific "acid wash" building code, but the Florida Building Code governs the structural integrity of pool shells. Acid washing removes the top layer of plaster, which can expose underlying cracks. If the pool shell is compromised, you may need to patch it before refilling, which adds repair costs.

**HVHZ and Wind:** While acid washing itself doesn't change your wind zone rating, the process often reveals damage that affects your pool's safety rating. If the acid wash exposes cracks in the coping or shell, you must repair them to meet code. This is critical if you are selling your home, as pool defects can affect insurance.

**Permits:** You generally do not need a building permit for an acid wash in Fort Lauderdale. However, if the process requires draining the pool and refilling it, you must ensure you are not violating local water usage restrictions. Broward County occasionally issues conservation orders during droughts. Always check current city water rules before draining.

**Insurance Impact:** A clean, well-maintained pool reduces liability risks. If your pool has rough, stained surfaces that could trap dirt or harbor bacteria, it might be a liability. While an acid wash doesn't directly lower your premium, a well-maintained pool helps avoid claims related to poor maintenance.

**HOA Restrictions:** Many Fort Lauderdale HOAs have strict rules about pool appearance and maintenance. Some prohibit the use of harsh chemicals near landscaping. Your contractor must follow these rules to avoid fines. Always get written approval from your HOA if you are unsure about the chemical process.

**Season:** The best time to acid wash is during the dry season, from **November to April**. High humidity in summer can slow the drying and neutralizing process. Also, heavy rain can wash away the neutralizing agents before they fully react. Avoid acid washing during peak hurricane season to prevent water contamination risks.

**My Safe Florida Home:** This program focuses on roof and structural hardening. While it does not directly fund pool acid washing, the inspection might reveal pool shell issues that need repair. If your pool shell is damaged, fixing it might qualify for a grant if it affects the home's structural safety.

## How to Save Money on Acid Washing in Fort Lauderdale

1. **Combine with a full water balance.** Don't just wash the pool; get a full water chemistry analysis. Many contractors offer a discount if you do a full service package. This prevents the need for another visit to fix the chemistry immediately after the wash.

2. **Schedule in the off-season.** November through March is the peak pool season, so prices are higher. However, late fall (October) or early spring (May) might offer better rates as contractors look for work before the summer rush or after the winter peak.

3. **Protect your landscaping yourself.** If you can move potted plants or cover delicate shrubs, you might save on labor costs. Contractors charge for the time spent protecting vegetation. If you handle the prep, they can focus on the acid application.

4. **Avoid "DIY" with muriatic acid.** Buying the acid yourself is cheap, but the risk is high. Improper handling can ruin your pool surface permanently or cause severe injury. The cost of a mistake far outweighs the **$400** you might save.

5. **Get multiple quotes.** Pricing varies by **15–25%** between contractors. Get at least three written estimates. Ensure they all include the same scope: drain, wash, neutralize, rinse, refill, and chemical balancing.

6. **Check for rebates.** While no specific rebate exists for acid washing, some pool supply stores offer discounts on chemicals if you buy in bulk. Use these savings to offset the labor cost.

7. **Bundle with other services.** If your pool needs a filter cleaning, pump repair, or tile cleaning, bundle the acid wash. Contractors often give a **10% discount** on the total job when multiple services are performed.

## When to Acid Wash Your Pool — Warning Signs

**Your pool is 5–7 years old and looks gray.** Plaster surfaces in Florida degrade faster due to UV exposure. If your pool has turned a dull gray or yellow despite regular cleaning, the surface is likely saturated with calcium and minerals. An acid wash is the only way to restore the white finish.

**You see white crusty deposits on the walls.** These are calcium scale deposits. They form when the water chemistry is unbalanced, often due to high pH or high calcium hardness. If you can't brush them off, they have bonded to the plaster and need chemical removal.

**The water stays cloudy after shock treatments.** If you've shocked the pool and the water remains cloudy with no visible algae, the cloudiness might be caused by microscopic mineral deposits. Acid washing clears these out effectively.

**You are preparing to sell your home.** A bright, white pool increases curb appeal and home value. A stained, gray pool looks neglected. An acid wash is a high-ROI improvement that makes the pool look new for a fraction of a resurfacing cost.

**Your tiles are stained above the waterline.** Acid washing removes stains from the waterline tile as well. If the tile is dull or has mineral rings, the acid treatment will clean it. This is a common issue in Fort Lauderdale due to the high mineral content in tap water.

## How to Hire a Pool Contractor in Fort Lauderdale

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Check at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified Pool and Spa Contractor (CPS) or a General Contractor (CGC) with pool experience.

2. **Confirm liability insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability coverage of at least **$1 million**. This protects you if the acid damages your landscaping or nearby property.

3. **Check for Broward County registration.** Ensure they have a local business tax receipt. This confirms they are a legitimate local business, not a transient operator.

4. **Get written estimates.** Ask for a detailed quote that includes: drain fees, acid cost, neutralizing agent, labor, refill water cost, and chemical balancing. Avoid vague "lump sum" quotes.

5. **Ask about their neutralization process.** A professional must test the water before discharge to ensure it meets Broward County environmental standards. If they don't mention this, walk away.

6. **Check reviews on Google.** Look for recent reviews mentioning "acid wash" specifically. Pay attention to comments about their care for landscaping and their professionalism.

7. **Avoid paying full upfront.** A typical deposit is **10–20%**. The balance is due upon completion and successful neutralization of the discharge water.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to acid wash a pool in Fort Lauderdale in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$450 and $1,200**. The average is around **$750**. The price depends on pool size, surface material, and the complexity of the site.

### Is acid washing safe for my pool?

Yes, if done by a professional. The acid removes the top layer of plaster, which is the damaged part. It reveals a fresh surface. However, over-washing can damage the pool, so it should only be done every **5–7 years** or when necessary.

### Can I do it myself?

Technically, you can buy muriatic acid, but it is dangerous. Improper handling can cause severe burns or ruin your pool. The risk of making a mistake is high, and the cost of fixing it far exceeds the labor savings.

### Does acid washing require a permit?

Generally, no. An acid wash is a maintenance task, not a structural renovation. However, you must comply with environmental regulations regarding water discharge. Always check with the City of Fort Lauderdale for any temporary water usage restrictions.

### How long does the process take?

The entire process takes **1–2 days**. Day 1 is for draining, washing, neutralizing, and rinsing. Day 2 is for refilling and balancing the water chemistry before you can swim again.

### Will it fix cracks in the pool?

No. Acid washing cleans the surface but does not repair structural cracks. If the acid wash reveals cracks, you will need a separate repair job, which costs extra.

Just remember: this is a chemical process that requires strict adherence to Broward County environmental rules. Hire a licensed professional who knows how to neutralize the water safely.
