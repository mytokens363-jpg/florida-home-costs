# How Much Does It Cost to Resurface a Pool in Davie? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Resurfacing an in-ground pool in Davie costs between **$6,500 and $14,500** for most standard sizes, with the average homeowner paying around **$9,500**. The biggest factors driving your price are pool surface area, material choice, and the condition of the underlying plaster or gunite shell.

Davie homeowners deal with specific challenges like high water tables and sandy soil that can affect pool stability. Your contractor needs to address these issues during the prep work, which adds to the cost but protects your investment.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$6,500 – $8,500** | Standard white plaster, small pool |
| Mid-Range | **$8,500 – $11,500** | Quartz or pebble finish, 16x32 ft |
| High-End | **$11,500 – $14,500+** | Aggregate finish, tile coping, repairs |

**Budget** covers standard white plaster on a smaller pool or simple geometry. It includes draining, acid wash, basic crack repair, and new plaster. This is the most common choice for rental properties or budget-conscious owners.

**Mid-range** includes quartz or aggregate finishes that are more durable than standard plaster. These surfaces resist staining better and typically last longer. Covers typical 16x32 foot pools with standard equipment.

**High-end** covers premium pebble finishes, custom tile work at the waterline, and significant structural repairs found during the drain. Includes upgraded plumbing or equipment if needed to match the new surface.

These prices reflect Davie market rates as of early 2026 and include labor, materials, disposal, and standard permits. They do not include major structural shell repairs or equipment upgrades unless specified in the scope.

## What Affects the Cost in Davie

### 1. Pool Size and Surface Area

Pool resurfacing is priced by the square footage of the interior shell. A small 12x24 foot pool has roughly 500 square feet of surface area. A standard 16x32 foot pool expands to about 900 square feet.

Material costs multiply directly with this area. **$6,500** covers the smaller end of the market. **$14,500** is for larger pools over 1,200 square feet or custom shapes like free-form designs common in Davie estates.

### 2. Material Choice

The finish you choose is the second biggest cost driver after size. Standard white plaster is the cheapest option at **$4.00–$6.00 per sq ft**. Quartz aggregate finishes jump to **$6.00–$9.00 per sq ft** because they include colored quartz aggregates.

Pebble finishes are the premium choice at **$7.00–$12.00 per sq ft**. They offer the best texture and durability but require more skilled application. Tile coping adds another **$15–$25 per linear foot** around the deck edge.

### 3. Plumbing and Equipment Condition

You cannot resurface a pool without checking the plumbing. If the pipes are leaking or corroded, you must fix them before the new plaster goes on. Repairing underground plumbing in Davie can add **$1,500–$3,000** to the project.

Older pools often need a pump or filter upgrade. New equipment costs **$1,000–$2,500**. Contractors often bundle this work to save on mobilization fees.

### 4. Structural Repairs

Sandy soil in Davie can shift over time. You may find cracks in the gunite shell that need more than just patching. Structural repairs involve rebar work and shotcrete application.

Minor cracks cost **$500–$1,000** to fix. Major structural work involving the floor or main wall can push costs over **$5,000** depending on the extent of the damage found during draining.

### 5. Draining and Disposal

You cannot resurface a pool without draining it. Disposing of the water requires care to avoid flooding neighbors or violating local ordinances. Davie has strict rules about where pool water can go due to the water table.

Disposal fees run **$300–$800**. Some contractors include this in the quote. Others charge it separately. Make sure you know who is responsible for hauling the water away.

### 6. Permits and Inspections

The City of Davie Building Department requires a permit for any pool resurfacing that involves structural work or plumbing changes. Permit fees range from **$250 to $600** depending on the total project valuation.

Inspections occur at two stages: plumbing pressure test before closing and final inspection after completion. Your contractor must pull the permit. Skipping permits risks fines and issues when you sell the home.

## Cost by Material Type

| Material | Cost per Sq Ft | Lifespan |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| White Plaster | **$4.00 – $6.00** | 7–10 years |
| Quartz Aggregate | **$6.00 – $9.00** | 10–15 years |
| Pebble Finish | **$7.00 – $12.00** | 15–25 years |
| Tile Finish | **$15.00 – $25.00** | 20+ years |
| Coping (Tile) | **$15.00 – $25.00** | 20+ years |

**White plaster** is the industry standard. It is smooth and bright but chips easily. It stains from minerals in the water quickly. Expect to resurface every 7 to 10 years with this option.

**Quartz aggregate** mixes small quartz stones into the plaster mix. It is harder and more resistant to stains. The texture is slightly rougher but feels better underfoot. This is the most popular mid-range choice in South Florida.

**Pebble finish** uses natural river pebbles for a luxurious look. It is the most durable option available for residential pools. The texture is the most natural to walk on. It reflects light beautifully in the pool water.

**Tile finish** involves installing ceramic or glass tiles directly on the pool surface. It is rare for the entire pool due to cost. It is usually reserved for the waterline coping. This offers the highest aesthetic value.

**Coping** refers to the stones or tiles around the pool edge. Replacing old coping during resurfacing is common. It prevents water from seeping under the deck. Expect to pay **$15.00–$25.00 per linear foot** for new tile coping.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code mandates specific safety barriers for all residential pools. Davie enforces the Florida Residential Pool and Spa Safety Act. This includes fence height, self-latching gates, and alarm requirements. Resurfacing contractors must ensure the new perimeter meets these safety standards.

**Soil and Water Table:** Davie sits on a high water table with sandy soil. When you drain a pool, the ground can shift or the pool can float if not properly anchored. Contractors must use proper bracing and counterweights. Failure to account for this can crack the new shell within months.

**Permits:** Apply through the City of Davie Building Department at 13600 Davie Road. Online permitting is available for some projects. Turnaround is typically 5–10 business days. Budget **$250–$600** for the fee. Inspections are mandatory before backfilling.

**Insurance Impact:** A resurfaced pool can lower your liability insurance risk. Insurers prefer pools with smooth surfaces that don't harbor bacteria or algae. However, you must have a working safety fence to qualify for discounts. Pool ownership generally adds **$200–$500** annually to your premium.

**Water Quality:** South Florida water is hard and high in minerals. This causes scale buildup on plaster faster than in other regions. You may need more frequent acid washing. Using a water softener or specialized filtration system helps extend the life of your new surface.

**Season:** Best time to resurface is November through April. Avoid the rainy season from May to October. Rain delays the curing process for plaster. High humidity in summer can cause the plaster to dry too slowly, leading to weak spots.

**My Safe Florida Home:** This program focuses on roof and window hardening. It does not typically cover pool resurfacing. However, improving pool safety barriers might qualify for other local incentives. Check with the City of Davie for specific safety grants.

## How to Save Money on Pool Resurfacing in Davie

1. **Get at least 4 written quotes.** Pool contractor pricing varies by 20–40% in Davie. On a **$10,000** job, that is **$2,000–$4,000** in potential savings. Ensure each quote itemizes materials, labor, permits, and disposal separately.

2. **Bundle repairs with the resurfacing.** Fixing plumbing or equipment while the pool is empty saves on labor mobilization. Contractors charge less to do plumbing work when the pool is already drained. This can save **$500–$1,000** on total costs.

3. **Choose quartz over pebble if budget is tight.** Quartz offers better durability than plaster for less money. The price difference between quartz (**$6.00–$9.00/sq ft**) and pebble (**$7.00–$12.00/sq ft**) is significant on larger pools.

4. **Schedule in the dry season.** November through April is slower for pool contractors. You have more leverage to negotiate. Contractors are less likely to have weather delays during these months compared to summer.

5. **Maintain water chemistry.** Proper balancing extends the life of your plaster. Spend **$50–$100 per month** on chemicals to prevent scaling or staining. A well-maintained pool can last 2–3 years longer than a neglected one.

## When to Resurface Your Pool — Warning Signs

**Your plaster is 10+ years old.** Standard plaster degrades faster in Florida heat and humidity. If it has not been resurfaced in over a decade, it is likely near the end of its life. Waiting longer often leads to structural cracks.

**The surface feels rough or abrasive.** Run your hand along the wall. If it feels like sandpaper, the plaster is eroding. This can scratch swimmers and damage swimwear. Rough surfaces also trap algae and bacteria.

**Visible stains that won't scrub off.** Iron or copper stains from water can penetrate the plaster. If acid washing does not remove them, the surface is compromised. Deep staining indicates the plaster is porous and failing.

**Frequent leaks or water loss.** If you lose more than 1 inch of water per week, you have a leak. Leaks often originate from cracks in the old surface. Resurfacing seals these minor leaks but major leaks need plumbing repair first.

**Discolored grout lines.** The lines between tiles or coping turning green or gray mean water is penetrating behind the surface. This compromises the bond between the shell and the finish. It is a sign of deep moisture intrusion.

## How to Hire a Pool Contractor in Davie

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Look for a Certified Pool Contractor (CPC) license. You can verify this at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). General contractors cannot do specialized pool work without this endorsement.

2. **Confirm active insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum $1 million) and workers comp. Call the insurer directly to verify the policy is active.

3. **Check for a Davie local business tax receipt.** This confirms they are registered locally, not a storm chaser from out of state. Davie requires this for all contractors working within city limits.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates** with line-item breakdowns — material brand, surface type, plumbing scope, permits, labor, timeline, and warranty.

5. **Check reviews on Google and BBB.** Look for patterns in complaints, not just star ratings. Pay attention to comments about cleanup and timeline adherence.

6. **Never pay more than 10% upfront.** Florida Statute 489.126 restricts contractor deposits. Payment should tie to milestones: deposit, drain complete, surface cure, final inspection passed.

7. **Get everything in writing.** Contract must include: scope, material specs with NOA numbers, dates, payment schedule, warranty terms, permit responsibility, and cleanup plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does pool resurfacing cost in Davie in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$6,500 and $14,500**. The average is around **$9,500**. Your actual cost depends on pool size, material choice, and whether structural repairs are needed.

### Do I need a permit to resurface my pool in Davie?

Yes, always. Apply through the City of Davie Building Department. Budget **$250–$600**. Inspections are required for plumbing and final completion. Never let a contractor skip the permit.

### How long does pool resurfacing take in Davie?

The actual work takes **3–5 days**. Draining and drying take **1–2 days**. Curing takes **7–14 days** before filling. Total timeline is usually **2–3 weeks** including permit processing.

### Does resurfacing increase home value in Davie?

A new pool surface recoups **50–60%** of its cost at resale. But the bigger value in Davie's market is safety and usability. A cracked pool is a liability. A new one is an asset.

### Can I drain my own pool to resurface it?

No, this is dangerous. The water table in Davie is high. Draining without proper bracing can cause the pool to pop out of the ground. Only licensed contractors should handle draining.

### Does homeowners insurance cover pool resurfacing?

No. Resurfacing is considered maintenance. Insurance only covers sudden damage like a tree falling on the pool. Wear and tear is never covered by standard policies.
