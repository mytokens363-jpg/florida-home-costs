# How Much Does It Cost to Resurface a Pool in Naples? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** A full pool resurface in Naples costs between **$6,500 and $20,000** for most homes, with the average homeowner paying around **$11,500**. The biggest factors driving your price are pool size, finish material choice, and Collier County permit requirements.

Naples homeowners have high standards for their outdoor living spaces. A tired, stained, or cracked pool surface lowers your property value and makes swimming uncomfortable. Resurfacing restores the look and extends the life of the shell for another decade or more.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$6,500 – $8,500** | White plaster, small pool |
| Mid-Range | **$8,500 – $12,500** | Quartz aggregate, avg size |
| High-End | **$12,500 – $20,000+** | Pebble finish, tile accents |

**Budget** gets you standard white plaster on a smaller pool with basic prep work. It meets minimum health codes but requires more maintenance over time.

**Mid-range** is what most Naples homeowners choose — a quartz aggregate finish. It offers better durability, color retention, and a smoother feel underfoot. Covers pools in the 300 to 500 sq ft surface area range.

**High-end** covers exposed aggregate pebble finishes or custom tile accents. Includes upgraded coping repairs, new drain covers, and enhanced chemical balancing systems. Best for larger estates or saltwater pools.

These prices reflect Naples market rates as of early 2026 and include water draining, surface prep, resurfacing materials, and cleanup. They do not include structural cracks repair or major plumbing replacement.

## What Affects the Cost in Naples

### 1. Pool Surface Area

Resurfacing is priced by the square foot of the interior surface (floor plus walls). A typical Naples in-ground pool has between 300 and 600 sq ft of surface area. At **$15–$35 per sq ft** installed, a 350 sq ft pool runs roughly **$6,500–$8,000** while a 600 sq ft pool pushes **$12,000–$15,000**.

### 2. Material Choice

This is the single biggest price lever you control. Standard white plaster is the most common option at **$12–$18 per sq ft** installed. Quartz aggregate — popular in Southwest Florida — jumps to **$20–$28**. Exposed aggregate pebble is the premium choice at **$28–$40 per sq ft** but lasts 15–20 years.

### 3. Drain and Refill Logistics

You cannot just drain a pool in Naples without checking local rules. Collier County requires that water be pumped to a sanitary sewer or approved disposal site, not the street or storm drain. Hiring a licensed pump-out service adds **$500–$1,500** to the project.

### 4. Surface Repairs

If the underlying gunite or concrete shell has cracks, the contractor must repair them before resurfacing. Hairline cracks are standard, but structural cracks require epoxy injection. This hidden cost adds **$1,000–$5,000** depending on severity.

### 5. Permits and Inspections

Collier County requires a building permit for any pool renovation involving structural changes or plumbing. Permit fees run **$400–$900** depending on project valuation. The process includes a pre-approval and final inspection. Your contractor should pull the permit — if they suggest skipping it, walk away.

### 6. Pool Equipment Upgrades

Many homeowners use a resurface to upgrade old equipment. Installing a new pump, filter, or heater during the drain adds **$2,000–$6,000**. It is often cheaper to bundle this work while the pool is empty than to call a separate technician later.

## Cost by Material Type

| Material | Cost per Sq Ft | Lifespan |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| White Plaster | **$12.00 – $18.00** | 7–10 years |
| Colored Plaster | **$15.00 – $22.00** | 7–10 years |
| Quartz Aggregate | **$20.00 – $28.00** | 10–15 years |
| Exposed Pebble | **$28.00 – $40.00** | 15–20 years |
| Tile Accent | **$25.00 – $45.00** | 20+ years |
| Coping Repair | **$30.00 – $50.00** | 10–15 years |

**White plaster** is the budget option. It is smooth but stains easily and can become rough over time. Best for rental properties or budget-conscious homeowners.

**Colored plaster** adds pigments to hide water line staining. It looks similar to standard plaster but costs slightly more. It does not significantly extend the lifespan compared to white.

**Quartz aggregate** is the sweet spot for Naples. Crushed quartz is mixed into the plaster, making it harder and more durable. It comes in various colors and resists algae growth better than plain plaster.

**Exposed pebble** is the premium choice. It exposes the pebble stones for a natural stone look. It is the most durable finish available, resisting chemicals and weather better than any other material.

**Tile accents** are often added along the water line to prevent calcium buildup. While not a full resurface, tile work adds **$2,000–$4,000** to the total project and protects the bond beam.

**Coping repair** covers the stone or concrete border around the pool. Salt air in Naples accelerates weathering on coping. Replacing damaged coping during resurfacing is **30–50% cheaper** than doing it later.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** Collier County follows the Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023). Key requirements include updated drain covers that meet VGB Act standards for anti-entrapment and specific fencing requirements if you have an alarm or cover.

**Water Disposal:** The Florida Department of Environmental Protection strictly regulates pool draining. You must neutralize chlorine and adjust pH to between 6.0 and 9.0 before draining. Dumping untreated water into storm drains is illegal and carries heavy fines.

**Permits:** Apply through Collier County Building Services Division. You can submit plans online or in person at the Collier County Administration Building. Turnaround is 7–14 business days for a standard pool permit. Budget **$400–$900** for the fee.

**Insurance Impact:** A new pool surface can lower liability risk, but saltwater systems may raise premiums. Some carriers offer discounts for new safety equipment like pool fences or alarms. Check with your agent before installing a salt system.

**HOA:** Many Naples communities restrict pool colors and equipment placement. Some prohibit exposed aggregate finishes that look too industrial. Get written HOA approval before signing a contractor agreement — changing materials mid-job is extremely expensive.

**Season:** Best time to resurface here is January through April — dry season, lower humidity, fewer storm delays. Avoid peak hurricane season (August–October). Rainy season (May–October) brings daily afternoon thunderstorms that delay curing.

**My Safe Florida Home:** If your home was built before 2008 and is homesteaded, you may qualify for a free wind inspection. While this does not cover pool resurfacing directly, it can identify fencing issues that affect insurance eligibility.

## How to Save Money on Pool Resurfacing in Naples

1. **Get at least 4 written quotes.** Contractor pricing varies by 25–40% for identical scope in Naples. On a **$12,000** job, that's **$3,000–$5,000** in potential savings. Make sure each quote itemizes materials, labor, drain, permits, and disposal separately.

2. **Schedule in dry season.** January through April is slower for pool contractors. You will have more leverage to negotiate and fewer weather delays. Some contractors offer **5–10%** off-season discounts to keep crews busy.

3. **Choose quartz over pebble if your budget is tight.** The savings between quartz (**$20–$28/sq ft**) and pebble (**$28–$40/sq ft**) can be **$2,000–$4,000** on a typical home. Quartz offers most of the durability at a lower price point.

4. **Bundle equipment upgrades.** Replacing your pump and filter during a resurface is **40–60% cheaper** than standalone projects. The combined efficiency improvements can also cut your electric bill by hundreds per year.

5. **Handle the drain yourself if allowed.** If you own a large lot with a sanitary sewer connection, you might be able to pump the water yourself after neutralizing chemicals. Check with Collier County first to avoid fines.

## When to Refinish Your Pool — Warning Signs

**Your pool is 10 years old.** Standard plaster in Southwest Florida degrades faster than in cooler climates due to intense UV, heat cycling, and salt air. A 10-year-old pool in Naples has taken more abuse than a 15-year-old pool in the Midwest.

**The surface feels rough.** Run your hand along the wall. If it feels like sandpaper, the plaster is breaking down. This creates a breeding ground for algae and bacteria. Rough surfaces also tear swimsuits and injure feet.

**Visible staining or discoloration.** Brown, green, or black spots that do not scrub off mean the plaster is porous. Chemicals are penetrating the shell and causing permanent damage. This usually indicates the seal has failed.

**Water leaks or high water bill.** If you are adding water constantly, the shell may be cracking. Leaks are common in older pools where the bond beam has shifted. Resurfacing alone won't fix a structural leak — you need a contractor to test first.

## How to Hire a Pool Contractor in Naples

1. **Verify their Florida license** at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified Pool and Spa Contractor (CPC) or a General Contractor (CGC) license.

2. **Confirm active insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum $1 million) and workers comp. Call the insurer directly to verify.

3. **Check for a Collier County local business tax receipt.** This confirms they are registered locally, not a storm chaser from out of state.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates** with line-item breakdowns — material brand, underlayment type, surface area, drain, permits, labor, timeline, and warranty.

5. **Check reviews on Google and BBB.** Look for patterns in complaints, not just star ratings. Pay attention to comments about cleanup and curing time.

6. **Never pay more than 10% upfront.** Florida Statute 489.126 restricts contractor deposits. Payment should tie to milestones: deposit, drain complete, mid-project, final inspection passed.

7. **Get everything in writing.** Contract must include: scope, material specs with NOA numbers, dates, payment schedule, warranty terms, permit responsibility, and cleanup plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to resurface a pool in Naples in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$6,500 and $20,000**. The average is around **$11,500**. Your actual cost depends on pool size, material choice, complexity, and whether structural repairs are needed.

### Do I need a permit to resurface my pool in Collier County?

Yes, usually. Apply through Collier County's Building Services Division. Budget **$400–$900**. One inspection required. Never let a contractor skip the permit — it can void your insurance and create title issues when you sell.

### How long does a pool resurface take in Naples?

Standard plaster takes **3–5 days** for an average pool. Quartz and pebble take **4–7 days**. Rainy season can add 2–5 days of weather delays. The pool must cure for 14–21 days before filling.

### Does a new pool surface increase home value in Naples?

A new pool surface recoups **50–60%** of its cost at resale. But the bigger value in Naples' market is usability — a home with a clean, smooth pool is dramatically easier to sell because buyers can see the condition clearly.

### Can I resurface my own pool in Florida?

Florida's homeowner exemption allows it if you own and occupy the home. But you still need permits, inspections, and must meet all health codes regarding drain covers and water disposal. Insurance companies may refuse to cover a DIY pool. For most people, it is not worth the risk.

### Does homeowners insurance cover pool resurfacing?

Normal wear and aging is never covered. Storm damage that cracks the shell might be covered minus your deductible. Maintenance issues like plaster deterioration are considered homeowner responsibility.
