# How Much Does It Cost to Install Hurricane Impact Windows in Palm Beach? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing hurricane impact windows in Palm Beach costs between **$450 and $950** per window for standard sizes, with the average homeowner spending around **$650** per unit. The final price depends heavily on window size, frame material, glass type, and Palm Beach County's strict building code requirements.

Palm Beach sits in a unique coastal environment where salt air, intense UV rays, and Category 5 hurricane threats drive specific construction standards. You cannot just buy off-the-shelf windows here. Every unit must be tested and certified to meet the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) requirements, which significantly impacts material costs and labor complexity.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$450 – $600** | Vinyl frames, single pane, 3x4 ft |
| Mid-Range | **$600 – $800** | Aluminum frames, dual pane, 4x5 ft |

**Budget** options usually feature vinyl frames with standard impact glass designed for smaller openings like 3x4 feet. These meet code but lack premium finishes or advanced energy efficiency coatings.

**Mid-range** covers aluminum frames with dual-pane Low-E glass, which is standard for most Palm Beach renovations. This tier handles larger openings like 4x5 feet and offers better thermal performance.

**High-end** includes wood-clad aluminum or full wood frames with premium Low-E glass and custom sizing. These are often found in luxury estates on the island or in Palm Beach Gardens.

These prices reflect installed costs in Palm Beach as of early 2026. They include materials, professional installation, disposal of old windows, and standard permit fees. They do not include structural framing repairs if rot is discovered during removal.

## What Affects the Cost in Palm Beach

### 1. Window Size and Shape

Window costs are rarely flat-rate; they scale with square footage. A standard 3x4 foot window is the baseline. Moving to a 4x5 foot unit increases the glass area by 66%, pushing the price up significantly. Custom shapes like arches, circles, or trapezoids require specialized framing and labor, adding **$200–$500** per unit.

### 2. Frame Material

Material choice is your primary cost driver. Vinyl frames are the most affordable option, ranging from **$150–$250** for the material alone. Aluminum frames cost **$200–$400** and offer a slimmer profile ideal for modern Palm Beach homes. Wood frames are the most expensive, often exceeding **$500** for the unit alone, though they provide a classic aesthetic.

### 3. Glass Technology

The glass itself is the most critical component. Standard impact glass is a single layer of laminated glass. Dual-pane impact glass adds an insulating air space between two layers of glass, improving energy efficiency but raising costs by **$100–$200** per window. Low-E coatings are standard in the mid-range to block UV heat, which is crucial in Palm Beach summers.

### 4. Labor Rates in Palm Beach

Labor in Palm Beach County is among the highest in Florida. Skilled installers charge **$75–$125 per hour**. The complexity of working on historic homes or second-story balconies on the island can increase labor time by 20–30%. High demand during the off-season (December–April) keeps rates stable, but storm damage backlogs can cause spikes.

### 5. Permits and Inspections

Palm Beach County requires a building permit for every impact window replacement. The permit fee typically ranges from **$50 to $150** per window depending on the project value. The process involves submitting product specifications and engineering drawings. Inspections are required before and after installation to ensure compliance with the Florida Building Code.

### 6. HVHZ and Product Approval

Palm Beach is in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone. Every window must have a Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) or Florida Product Approval. This certification ensures the unit can withstand 170+ mph winds and missile impact. Products without this certification are illegal to install, limiting your choices to certified manufacturers and adding a premium to the price.

## Cost by Material Type

| Material | Cost per Window | Lifespan |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Vinyl | **$450 – $650** | 20–30 years |
| Aluminum | **$600 – $850** | 30–40 years |
| Wood-Clad | **$750 – $950** | 40–50 years |
| Full Wood | **$900 – $1,200** | 40+ years |
| Sliding | **$550 – $800** | 20–30 years |
| Casement | **$500 – $750** | 25–35 years |

**Vinyl** frames are the most popular choice for their balance of cost and performance. They require no painting and resist salt air corrosion well. Best for standard rectangular openings in single-family homes.

**Aluminum** frames offer a sleek, modern look with a thinner sightline. They are incredibly strong and handle the high wind loads of Palm Beach perfectly. Expect to pay more than vinyl but get a longer lifespan and better durability.

**Wood-clad** frames feature an aluminum exterior for weather protection and a wood interior for aesthetics. This is a top choice for historic districts and luxury properties. The cost is higher, but the curb appeal and insulation properties are superior.

**Full wood** windows are the premium option, often found in custom builds. They require regular maintenance like painting or staining to prevent rot from humidity. They offer the best thermal performance but come with the highest price tag.

**Sliding** impact windows are often used for larger openings or patio access. They are slightly more expensive than fixed or casement windows due to the complex track hardware required for sliding mechanisms.

**Casement** windows crank open and seal tightly, offering excellent wind resistance. They are a common choice for bedrooms and living areas where ventilation is needed without compromising security.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) mandates that all impact windows in Palm Beach County must pass specific testing protocols. This includes large missile impact tests and cyclic pressure tests. The glass must be laminated with a PVB interlayer to hold together if shattered.

**Hurricane Zone:** Palm Beach County is entirely within the HVHZ. This means your windows must be certified for wind speeds up to 180 mph. You cannot use standard "storm-rated" windows from other states; they must have the specific Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval number stamped on the glass.

**Permits:** Apply through the Palm Beach County Building Department online portal. The process usually takes 7–14 business days. You will need to submit the manufacturer's NOA, engineering drawings, and a site plan. Budget **$50–$150** per window for permit fees. Two inspections are standard: rough-in and final.

**Insurance Impact:** Installing impact windows can lower your wind insurance premium by **15–45%**. Many carriers offer a "wind mitigation" credit for impact-resistant glazing. This can save homeowners **$500–$1,500** annually, paying for the windows over time. Some policies even waive the hurricane deductible if impact protection is installed.

**HOA:** Palm Beach and Palm Beach Gardens have strict HOA rules regarding window color, frame style, and visibility. You cannot simply install aluminum frames if your deed requires wood. Get written approval from your HOA architectural review committee before purchasing materials to avoid fines or forced removal.

**Season:** The best time to install windows is during the dry season, **December through April**. Rain delays are minimal, and contractors are more available. Avoid the peak of hurricane season (August–October) when contractors are overwhelmed with storm damage repairs and prices may spike.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners with pre-2008 homes may qualify for free inspections and grants up to **$10,000** for impact window upgrades. The program specifically targets hurricane hardening improvements. Check eligibility through the program's website before signing a contract.

## How to Save Money on Impact Window Installation in Palm Beach

1. **Get at least 4 detailed quotes.** Pricing for impact windows varies by **20–30%** across Palm Beach. On a **$20,000** project, that is **$4,000–$6,000** in potential savings. Ensure each quote includes the NOA numbers, glass specifications, and permit fees.

2. **Bundle the project.** Replacing all windows at once is significantly cheaper per unit than doing them room by room. Contractors offer **10–15%** discounts for full-house jobs because they can optimize material ordering and crew scheduling.

3. **Choose aluminum over wood if possible.** Aluminum frames are **$200–$400** cheaper per window than wood-clad options. They offer similar wind resistance and require less maintenance in the salt air environment.

4. **Verify insurance discounts upfront.** Ask your insurance agent for a wind mitigation form before signing. Some carriers require the work to be completed and inspected before the discount applies. Get the paperwork from the contractor immediately after the final inspection.

5. **Look for rebates and grants.** Check the My Safe Florida Home program for matching grants. Some utility companies in Palm Beach also offer rebates for energy-efficient impact windows with Low-E coatings. These can cover **$500–$2,000** of your total cost.

## When to Replace Your Windows — Warning Signs

**Your windows are 20+ years old.** Most impact windows have a lifespan of 20–30 years. Older units may have failed seals, resulting in foggy glass or moisture buildup between panes. The structural integrity of the frame may also be compromised by rot or corrosion.

**You feel drafts or hear noise.** If you can feel air leaking around the frame or hear the wind whistle during a storm, your seals are broken. This compromises the impact rating and your home's energy efficiency.

**The glass is scratched or damaged.** Even small cracks or deep scratches in the laminated glass can reduce its ability to withstand impact. If a piece of debris hits the window, a compromised pane might shatter.

**Your insurance premium is skyrocketing.** If your insurer is raising rates or refusing to renew because of window age, replacement is urgent. A new impact window installation can stabilize your premium and prevent policy cancellation.

## How to Hire a Window Contractor in Palm Beach

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Check for a Certified Window and Door Contractor (CWC) or General Contractor (CGC) license at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). This is non-negotiable in Florida.

2. **Confirm insurance coverage.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability and workers comp. Call the carrier to verify the policy is active and covers the full project value.

3. **Check for Palm Beach County registration.** Ensure they have a local business tax receipt. This distinguishes legitimate local businesses from out-of-state storm chasers.

4. **Demand product certifications.** Ask for the Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval number for every window model. Verify these numbers on the official state database before signing.

5. **Get written warranties.** The contract must specify the manufacturer's warranty on the glass and frame, plus the installer's warranty on labor. Look for at least a 10-year labor warranty.

6. **Never pay 100% upfront.** Florida law limits deposits. Payment should follow milestones: deposit, delivery of materials, rough-in inspection, and final inspection.

7. **Review past projects.** Ask to see photos of similar installations in Palm Beach. Drive by the homes if possible to check the finish quality and how the windows sit in the frame.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install impact windows in Palm Beach in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$450 and $950** per window. The average cost is around **$650** per unit. Prices vary based on size, frame material, and glass type.

### Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Palm Beach County?

Yes, always. You must apply through the Palm Beach County Building Department. Permit fees range from **$50 to $150** per window. Inspections are mandatory to verify code compliance.

### How long does the installation take?

A standard 10-window replacement takes **2–4 days** for a crew of two. Complex installations with custom framing or historic restoration can take **5–7 days**. Weather delays are rare in the dry season.

### Will I get an insurance discount?

Yes, installing impact windows can lower your wind premium by **15–45%**. Many carriers offer a wind mitigation credit. This can save **$500–$1,500** annually on your bill.

### Can I install the windows myself?

Florida law allows homeowners to install windows on their own primary residence. However, you still need permits, inspections, and must meet all HVHZ requirements. Most insurance companies will not honor a self-installed impact window claim.

### Do impact windows come in different colors?

Yes, frames come in white, tan, bronze, black, and custom colors. Aluminum frames can be powder-coated in any color. Wood frames can be painted or stained. Check your HOA rules for color restrictions.
