# How Much Does It Cost to Install Hurricane Impact Windows in Pembroke Pines? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing hurricane impact windows in Pembroke Pines costs between **$8,500 and $22,000** for a typical single-family home. The average homeowner pays around **$14,500** for a full home replacement of 15 to 20 windows.

Pembroke Pines sits squarely in Broward County's High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ). This means your windows must pass the strictest testing in Florida, including large and small missile impact tests. These requirements drive up material costs compared to non-hurricane zones, but they also provide critical protection for your family and home.

**Budget** projects typically cover smaller homes or those replacing only the most critical windows. These use standard vinyl frames with single-pane impact glazing. They meet code but lack advanced insulation features.

**Mid-range** is the most common choice for Pembroke Pines homeowners. It includes 15 to 18 windows with dual-pane impact glass, better thermal breaks, and standard aluminum or vinyl frames. This range covers most 1,500 to 2,200 sq ft homes.

These prices reflect Pembroke Pines market rates as of early 2026. They include materials, professional installation, disposal of old windows, and Broward County permit fees. They do not include structural repairs to the rough opening if rot or damage is found.

## What Affects the Cost in Pembroke Pines

### 1. Number and Size of Windows

Window costs are driven by the total linear footage and the number of openings. A standard 36x48 inch window costs significantly less per unit than a 72x84 inch sliding door. Replacing 15 standard windows costs roughly **$12,000**, while replacing 10 windows plus two large sliding doors can push the total to **$18,000**.

### 2. Frame Material Choice

Vinyl frames are the most popular option in South Florida due to their resistance to salt air and humidity. They cost **$450–$650 per window** installed. Aluminum frames are stronger and slimmer but cost **$600–$900 per window**. Wood frames are rare in this zone due to maintenance requirements and cost **$900–$1,500 per window**.

### 3. Glass Type and Features

Standard impact glass is a single pane of laminated glass. Upgrading to dual-pane impact glass adds a second layer with an air gap for better insulation. This upgrade adds **$150–$300 per window**. Adding Low-E coatings to reflect solar heat can add another **$100–$200 per window**, which is crucial for Pembroke Pines' intense summer sun.

### 4. Labor Rates in Broward County

Specialized impact window installers in Broward County charge **$75–$110 per hour**. This is higher than the national average due to the specialized training required for HVHZ products. Demand spikes after storm seasons, potentially raising labor costs by another 15% for emergency replacements.

### 5. Permit and Inspection Fees

Broward County requires a building permit for any window replacement that changes the opening size or involves structural changes. Permit fees typically range from **$150 to $400** depending on the project value. The process includes a plan review and a final inspection to verify the installation meets code.

### 6. Window Shape and Complexity

Standard rectangular windows are the most affordable to install. Custom shapes like arches, circles, or trapezoids require custom manufacturing and specialized framing. These can increase the cost by **20–40%** per unit. Replacing windows in stucco homes often requires additional patching and painting, adding **$500–$1,500** to the total project.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) mandates that all impact windows in Broward County must pass the Miami-Dade NOA (Notice of Acceptance) process. This involves rigorous testing for wind pressure and missile impact. Your windows must display a permanent label showing the NOA number.

**Hurricane Zone:** Pembroke Pines is in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone. This requires windows to withstand 150 mph winds and impact from a 9-pound 2x4 lumber projectile fired at 34 feet per second. Standard windows sold in other states will not meet this requirement and cannot be installed legally.

**Permits:** Broward County handles permits through their ePlan portal or the Building Department in Davie. You need a permit if you are replacing windows in a new construction or if the opening size changes. The permit fee is usually **$150–$400**. Inspections are required after installation to verify the framing and sealing.

**Insurance Impact:** Installing impact windows is one of the best ways to lower your insurance premium in Florida. Many carriers offer a **20–30% discount** on the windstorm portion of your premium for homes with impact-rated openings. Some insurers may even waive the separate wind deductible if the entire home is fortified.

**HOA:** Many Pembroke Pines communities have strict Homeowners Association rules regarding window color, frame material, and exterior appearance. Some HOAs require specific colors or prohibit certain frame styles. Always get written HOA approval before ordering windows to avoid costly rework.

**Season:** The best time to install windows is during the dry season, from November to April. Rain delays are common in the summer rainy season (May–October). Installing during the dry season ensures faster curing of sealants and fewer weather-related interruptions.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners in Pembroke Pines may qualify for the My Safe Florida Home program. This offers free wind inspections and grants up to **$10,000** for hardening improvements. Impact windows are an eligible expense for the grant portion if they meet specific criteria.

## How to Save Money on Impact Window Installation in Pembroke Pines

1. **Get at least 4 written quotes.** Pricing for impact windows varies by 25–35% between contractors. On a **$14,000** project, this difference is **$3,500–$5,000**. Ensure every quote includes the exact same window model, glass type, and installation scope.

2. **Bundle windows and doors.** Contractors often offer a discount when replacing multiple units at once. Replacing 15 windows and 2 doors together might save **10–15%** compared to doing them as separate projects. The labor setup cost is amortized across more units.

3. **Choose standard sizes.** Custom shapes cost significantly more. Stick to standard widths and heights whenever possible. If your existing openings are non-standard, consider blocking them to standard sizes before ordering new windows to save on manufacturing costs.

4. **Schedule during off-peak season.** November through March is the busiest time for window installers in South Florida. Scheduling for late spring or early summer might yield **5–10%** lower labor rates, provided you are not in a rush due to an incoming storm.

5. **Check for My Safe Florida Home grants.** If your home is homesteaded and built before 2008, you may qualify for a grant that covers up to **$10,000** of the cost. This program is funded by the state and can drastically reduce your out-of-pocket expense.

## When to Replace Your Windows — Warning Signs

**Your windows are 20+ years old.** Most impact windows manufactured before 2005 do not meet current HVHZ standards. Even if they look fine, they may not provide adequate protection in a major storm. Older windows also often lack modern energy-efficient features.

**You see cracks or delamination.** Check the glass for cracks or a cloudy film between the panes. This indicates the seal has failed. Delamination in impact glass can compromise its structural integrity, making it unsafe during high winds.

**High energy bills.** If your AC is running constantly, your windows might be leaking air. Old impact windows often have poor thermal breaks. Upgrading to modern dual-pane impact glass can reduce cooling costs by **15–25%** in Pembroke Pines.

**Difficulty opening or closing.** Sticking windows often mean the frame has warped due to heat or humidity. In a hurricane, you need windows that open easily for ventilation before the storm and close tightly for protection.

**Visible water intrusion.** Water stains on the interior trim or walls near windows indicate a failed seal. This leads to mold growth and wood rot. Impact windows must be installed with proper flashing to prevent water intrusion.

## How to Hire a Window Contractor in Pembroke Pines

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Check for a Certified or Registered window and door contractor license at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). Look for the specific classification for window and door installation.

2. **Confirm active insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum $1 million) and workers comp. Call the insurer to verify the policy is active and covers work in Broward County.

3. **Check for a Broward County business tax receipt.** This confirms the contractor is registered locally. Storm chasers from out of state often lack local registration and may disappear after the job.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates.** The estimate must list the window brand, glass type (NOA number), frame material, installation details, and warranty terms. Do not accept a verbal quote or a one-line price.

5. **Check reviews on Google and BBB.** Look for specific mentions of impact window installation. General contractors may not have the same expertise as specialized window companies.

6. **Never pay more than 10% upfront.** Florida Statute 489.126 limits contractor deposits. Payments should be tied to milestones: deposit, delivery of materials, installation completion, and final inspection passed.

7. **Get everything in writing.** The contract must include the NOA numbers for every window model, the specific installation method, cleanup responsibilities, and the warranty period for both glass and labor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install hurricane impact windows in Pembroke Pines in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$8,500 and $22,000** for a full home installation. The average cost is around **$14,500**. Your specific price depends on the number of windows, frame material, and glass features you choose.

### Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Broward County?

Yes, usually. If you are replacing windows and the opening size changes, you need a permit. The permit fee is typically **$150–$400**. Even if the opening doesn't change, many contractors pull a permit to ensure the work is inspected and compliant with HVHZ codes.

### How long does it take to install impact windows in Pembroke Pines?

A standard home with 15 to 20 windows takes **3 to 5 days** to install. Larger homes with custom shapes or sliding doors can take **7 to 10 days**. Rainy season weather can add 1–3 days to the timeline.

### Do impact windows lower my insurance premium in Florida?

Yes, significantly. Most Florida insurers offer a **20–30% discount** on the windstorm portion of your premium for homes with impact-rated windows. Some carriers may also waive the separate wind deductible, saving you thousands in the event of a claim.

### Can I install impact windows myself in Florida?

No. Florida law requires that impact window installation be performed by a licensed contractor. Homeowners cannot legally install impact windows themselves if the work requires a permit. Additionally, DIY installation often voids the manufacturer's warranty.

### What is the difference between impact windows and storm shutters?

Impact windows are permanently installed and provide continuous protection without the need to deploy them. Storm shutters are temporary and must be installed before a storm hits. Impact windows generally offer better security, aesthetics, and convenience, though they have a higher upfront cost.
