# How Much Does It Cost to Install Hurricane Impact Windows in Boca Raton? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing hurricane impact windows in Boca Raton costs between **$35 and $65 per square foot**, or roughly **$800 to $1,800 per window** for standard sizes. The average homeowner with a 1,800 sq ft home spends around **$15,000 to $22,000** for a full replacement.

Boca Raton sits in Palm Beach County, which has specific building code requirements that differ slightly from Miami-Dade but are still rigorous. These windows are engineered to withstand flying debris and high wind pressures without shattering.

They are a major investment, but they also protect your home from storm surge damage and significantly lower your wind mitigation insurance credits.

**Budget** options typically feature vinyl frames with a single layer of laminated glass. They meet the basic Florida Building Code requirements for wind and impact but offer less insulation and a simpler aesthetic.

**Mid-range** is the most popular choice for Boca Raton homes. These units usually feature dual-pane glass with Low-E coatings for energy efficiency. Frames are often reinforced vinyl or powder-coated aluminum.

**High-end** installations use premium aluminum or steel frames with triple-pane glass and custom colors to match historic or luxury home aesthetics. They often include decorative muntins or grids that mimic traditional divided light windows.

These price ranges reflect Boca Raton market rates for early 2026. They include professional installation, disposal of old windows, and necessary permit fees. They do not include structural repairs to the framing if rot or damage is found during removal.

## What Affects the Cost in Boca Raton

### 1. Window Size and Configuration

Window costs are not linear. A standard 36x48 inch window costs significantly less than a large sliding patio door or a custom arched window. Custom shapes require specialized glass cutting and framing, adding **$200–$500** per unit. Large expanses of glass also require thicker glass panes to meet wind load requirements, driving up material costs.

### 2. Frame Material Choice

Vinyl frames are the most affordable option, ranging from **$300–$600** for the frame itself. They are low maintenance and energy efficient but have limited color options. Aluminum frames cost **$500–$900** more per window. They are stronger, slimmer, and allow for more design flexibility, which is popular in Boca Raton's modern and Mediterranean styles.

### 3. Glass Technology

Standard single-pane impact glass is the entry point. Upgrading to dual-pane or triple-pane glass adds **$150–$400** per window but drastically improves energy efficiency. Low-E coatings reflect heat, keeping your AC running cheaper during Florida summers. This is a critical feature for Boca Raton homes where cooling costs are high.

### 4. Labor Rates in Palm Beach County

Labor for window installation in Palm Beach County is higher than the national average. Skilled installers charge **$65–$95 per hour**. The complexity of removing old windows without damaging stucco or masonry adds time to the job. High-end homes with intricate details require more careful handling, pushing labor costs up.

### 5. Permit and Inspection Fees

Palm Beach County requires a building permit for window replacement if it involves structural changes or new openings. The permit fee is generally **$100–$300** depending on the total project value. The county requires a plan review for larger projects or new openings, adding time to the process.

### 6. Stucco and Masonry Work

Many Boca Raton homes have stucco or brick exteriors. Removing old windows often damages the surrounding stucco. Repairing and patching stucco to match the original finish adds **$1,000–$3,000** to the project. This is often an overlooked cost that surprises homeowners.

## Cost by Window Type

| Window Type | Cost Per Window | Glass Type |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| Single-Hung | **$800 – $1,100** | Single Pane Impact |
| Double-Hung | **$900 – $1,300** | Dual Pane Impact |
| Sliding Patio Door | **$1,800 – $2,500** | Dual/ Triple Pane |
| Casement | **$1,000 – $1,400** | Dual Pane Impact |
| Bay/Bow Window | **$3,500 – $5,000** | Custom Config |
| Fixed Picture | **$700 – $1,000** | Single Pane Impact |

**Single-hung windows** are the most basic impact option. The bottom sash moves up, while the top stays fixed. They are cost-effective for bedrooms and bathrooms where ventilation is less critical.

**Double-hung windows** allow both sashes to move. They provide better airflow and are standard for living areas. The dual-pane upgrade is highly recommended here to reduce heat gain during the hot Florida summer.

**Sliding patio doors** are the most expensive single units. They require heavy-duty frames to support the large glass area. They are essential for patios and lanais but come with a higher price tag due to the size and weight.

**Casement windows** crank open and seal tightly against the frame. They offer the best energy efficiency because they don't have moving parts in the center of the glass. They are excellent for hurricane protection due to their tight seal.

**Bay and bow windows** are complex custom structures that extend outward from the home. They require custom framing and specialized glass angles. These are premium features that significantly increase the total project cost.

**Fixed picture windows** do not open. They are great for views and light but offer no ventilation. They are the cheapest impact option but are often used in combination with operable windows.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** Palm Beach County enforces the Florida Building Code 8th Edition. All impact windows must pass ASTM E1886 and E1996 testing. This means they must withstand a large missile impact test and a cyclic pressure test simulating hurricane winds. The glass must be laminated with a polyvinyl butyral (PVB) interlayer.

**HVHZ Status:** Boca Raton is in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) for wind speed ratings, though not as strictly regulated as Miami-Dade for product approval. However, products must still have Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA. Your contractor must provide the approval number for every window installed.

**Permits:** Apply through the Palm Beach County Building Department online portal or in person at the Administration Building. The process takes 2–4 weeks for standard replacements. You need a site plan showing window locations and sizes. The permit fee is based on the total construction value.

**Insurance Impact:** This is the biggest financial benefit. Installing impact windows can lower your windstorm deductible from **2% to 1%** or even eliminate it in some policies. Many carriers offer a **20–30%** discount on the wind portion of your premium. Some insurers now offer "wind mitigation" credits specifically for impact glazing.

**HOA Restrictions:** Boca Raton has many deed-restricted communities with strict architectural guidelines. Colors, grid patterns, and frame materials are often regulated. You must submit a request for architectural approval before ordering windows. Violating these rules can lead to fines or forced removal.

**Season:** The best time to install windows is during the dry season, **November through April**. Rain delays can stall projects in the rainy season (May–October). High humidity can also affect the curing of sealants used during installation.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners with homesteaded properties built before 2008 can apply for free wind inspections. If your home qualifies, you may receive a matching grant of up to **$10,000** for impact window upgrades. This is a significant rebate that can offset a large portion of the cost.

## How to Save Money on Impact Window Installation in Boca Raton

1. **Bundle the entire house.** Contractors offer volume discounts. Replacing all windows at once is often **10–15% cheaper** per unit than doing a few rooms at a time. It also reduces mobilization costs and setup fees.

2. **Stick to standard sizes.** Custom sizes require special ordering and longer lead times. If you can work with standard dimensions, you save on glass cutting and framing costs. Measure your existing openings carefully before ordering.

3. **Choose vinyl frames if allowed.** Vinyl is cheaper than aluminum and requires less maintenance. If your HOA allows it, vinyl can save **$200–$400** per window. It is a practical choice for the Florida climate.

4. **Check for rebates and grants.** Look into the My Safe Florida Home program immediately. Some local utilities or community development grants may also offer incentives for energy-efficient impact windows.

5. **Schedule in the off-season.** Window installers are busiest in the spring and fall. Scheduling in the summer (outside of peak hurricane prep) might yield a **5–10%** discount. Contractors are often more willing to negotiate when work is slower.

6. **Verify insurance credits first.** Talk to your insurance agent before signing a contract. Knowing your exact potential savings helps you budget. Some policies require specific documentation to apply the discount, so get that paperwork ready.

## When to Replace Your Windows — Warning Signs

**Your windows are 20+ years old.** Older windows often lack the laminated glass required for impact resistance. They may have failed their wind tests or simply degrade over time. Florida's UV exposure breaks down seals and frames faster than in other states.

**You hear wind noise during storms.** If you hear whistling or see drafts during a tropical storm, your windows are not sealed properly. This indicates a failure in the weatherstripping or frame integrity. It puts your home at risk during high winds.

**Condensation between panes.** Double-pane windows that fog up have lost their seal. This reduces insulation value and indicates the window is nearing the end of its life. While not a direct safety hazard, it signals inefficiency.

**Difficulty opening or closing.** Stuck windows are a safety hazard during an evacuation. If you cannot open a window quickly during an emergency, it is a liability. Corrosion from salt air in Boca Raton often causes this issue.

**Visible cracks or chips in glass.** Even small cracks compromise the structural integrity of impact glass. A small chip can spread into a catastrophic failure under wind pressure. Replace any glass with visible damage immediately.

## How to Hire a Window Contractor in Boca Raton

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Check at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified or Registered Window, Door, and Sheet Metal Contractor (CWC) license. A general contractor license is not sufficient for window installation.

2. **Confirm product approval.** Ask for the Florida Product Approval number or Miami-Dade NOA for the specific window model. Verify this number on the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation website.

3. **Check for insurance.** Ensure they have general liability and workers compensation. Request a certificate of insurance directly from their provider. Do not rely on a verbal claim.

4. **Get written estimates.** A good quote details the brand, model, glass type, frame material, and labor. It should also include permit fees, disposal costs, and warranty terms.

5. **Review past work.** Ask for photos of recent installations in Boca Raton or nearby Palm Beach County. Look for clean cuts, proper flashing, and neat stucco repairs.

6. **Avoid upfront deposits over 10%.** Florida law limits deposits to 10% for residential projects. Payments should be tied to milestones: deposit, delivery, installation, and final inspection.

7. **Get a detailed contract.** The contract must specify the exact window models, dimensions, colors, and the warranty coverage. It should also state who is responsible for obtaining permits and scheduling inspections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install hurricane impact windows in Boca Raton in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$800 and $2,200 per window**, averaging **$15,000 to $22,000** for a full home replacement. The cost depends on size, material, and glass type.

### Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Palm Beach County?

Yes, usually. If you are replacing windows in the same opening, a permit might not be needed, but new openings or structural changes require one. Budget **$100–$300** for the fee.

### How long does installation take?

A typical 10-window replacement takes **2–4 days**. Large homes with 20+ windows may take **5–7 days**. Weather delays are common during the rainy season.

### Will my insurance premium go down?

Yes. Impact windows often qualify for a **20–30%** discount on the wind portion of your premium. Some carriers may lower your deductible to **1%** or even **0%**.

### Can I install windows myself in Florida?

Technically, yes, if you own and occupy the home. However, you must pull permits, pass inspections, and ensure the installation meets code. Insurance companies may deny claims if the work is not done by a licensed professional.

### What is the difference between impact windows and hurricane shutters?

Impact windows are permanent and require no maintenance. Shutters are removable and require storage. Impact windows add to home value and convenience, while shutters are a lower upfront cost but less convenient.
