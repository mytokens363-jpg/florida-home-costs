# How Much Does It Cost to Install Hurricane Shutters in Boca Raton? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing hurricane shutters in Boca Raton costs between **$15.00 and $45.00 per square foot**, with a typical full-home installation ranging from **$12,000 to $35,000**. The final price depends heavily on the shutter type you choose, the number of openings, and Palm Beach County's strict wind-load requirements.

Boca Raton sits right on the coast, making it a target for high-velocity winds during the Atlantic hurricane season. This means your shutters must be engineered to withstand specific wind pressures that inland Florida homes never face.

You cannot just buy generic panels off a shelf. Every product must carry a Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA to pass inspection and satisfy your insurance carrier.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$12,000 – $18,000** | Accordion or roll-down, 8–10 openings |

**Budget** projects usually involve accordion shutters or basic roll-down systems covering a standard 2,000 sq ft home. These are functional, code-compliant, and the most common choice for cost-conscious homeowners.

**Mid-range** covers colonial shutters or upgraded Bahama styles. These offer better aesthetics and security but require more manual labor to install and operate. They fit well in Boca's historic and upscale neighborhoods.

**High-end** involves automated roll-down systems with battery backup and custom fabrications for unique window shapes. This tier often includes smart-home integration and premium finishes that blend seamlessly with modern architecture.

These estimates include materials, professional installation, necessary permits, and cleanup. They assume standard residential window sizes. Custom architectural openings or sliding glass doors will push costs toward the higher end of these ranges.

## What Affects the Cost in Boca Raton

### 1. Shutter Type Selection

The style of shutter you choose drives the majority of your cost. Accordion shutters are the most affordable, often costing **$15–$25 per sq ft**. Colonial and Bahama shutters are mid-tier at **$25–$35 per sq ft**. Rolling shutters with motors are premium, running **$35–$45 per sq ft**.

### 2. Number of Openings

Boca Raton homes vary wildly in window count. A typical single-family home has 15 to 25 openings. Each additional window or door adds a fixed cost for tracks, brackets, and labor. Adding five extra openings can increase your total bill by **$2,000–$4,000**.

### 3. Palm Beach County Permit Fees

Palm Beach County requires a building permit for all permanent shutter installations. Permit fees are based on the total project value, typically ranging from **$150 to $600**. The process involves a plan review and a final inspection to verify wind-load ratings.

### 4. Window and Door Geometry

Standard rectangular windows are the easiest and cheapest to shutter. Arched tops, bay windows, and sliding glass doors require custom framing and specialized tracks. Complex shapes can add **$200–$500 per opening** compared to standard sizes.

### 5. Automation and Power Sources

Manual shutters are the baseline. Motorized roll-down shutters require electrical wiring, a motor unit, and often a backup battery system. This automation adds **$1,500–$3,000** to a project but offers convenience and safety during sudden storms.

### 6. HVHZ and Building Code Compliance

Boca Raton is in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone. Every shutter system must be tested and rated for the specific wind speed of your location. Non-compliant products will fail inspection and void your insurance. This requirement limits you to certified manufacturers, keeping prices stable but higher than national averages.

## Cost by Shutter Type

| Shutter Type | Cost per Sq Ft | Lifespan |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Accordion | **$15.00 – $25.00** | 20–25 years |
| Colonial | **$25.00 – $35.00** | 20–30 years |
| Bahama | **$30.00 – $40.00** | 25–30 years |
| Roll-Down (Manual) | **$35.00 – $42.00** | 20–25 years |
| Roll-Down (Motorized) | **$40.00 – $50.00** | 25–30 years |
| Storm Panels | **$10.00 – $18.00** | 10–15 years |

**Accordion shutters** fold into a compact box when not in use. They are quick to deploy, covering large areas like sliding glass doors efficiently. They are the most popular choice for Boca homeowners seeking a balance of cost and ease.

**Colonial shutters** swing open and close like traditional windows. They provide excellent security and a classic look. They are heavier and require more robust framing, which adds to the installation cost.

**Bahama shutters** are hinged at the top and prop open with arms. They offer great ventilation while closed and are aesthetically pleasing for coastal homes. They are slightly more expensive than colonial styles due to the hinge mechanism.

**Roll-down shutters** retract into a housing above the window. Manual versions require cranking, while motorized versions operate with a switch. They are the most secure and offer the best view when retracted, but the highest upfront cost.

**Storm panels** are removable aluminum or steel sheets stored on-site. They are the cheapest option but require significant labor to install and remove before and after every storm. They also block your view entirely when deployed.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) mandates strict wind-load calculations for Palm Beach County. Your shutters must be rated for the specific wind speed zone where your home sits. This often means 150 mph or higher depending on your exact location near the coast.

**Hurricane Zone:** Boca Raton is in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ). This zone requires products to pass rigorous testing, including missile impact tests. You cannot install standard shutters; they must have a valid Florida Product Approval number or Miami-Dade NOA.

**Permits:** Apply through the Palm Beach County Development Services Center. You can submit plans online via their portal or in person. The typical turnaround is 5–10 business days. Budget **$200–$500** for the permit fee, which covers plan review and the final inspection.

**Insurance Impact:** Installing approved hurricane shutters can significantly lower your wind insurance premium. Many carriers offer discounts of **15–25%** for homes with impact-resistant openings or approved shutters. This savings can offset installation costs over time.

**HOA Restrictions:** Many Boca Raton communities have strict architectural guidelines. Some HOAs prohibit visible accordion shutters or require specific colors. Always get written HOA approval before purchasing. Ignoring these rules can lead to fines or forced removal.

**Season:** Hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30. Demand for installers spikes in late summer and early fall. Prices often rise, and scheduling delays occur. Booking your project in the winter or early spring (December–April) ensures better pricing and faster installation.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners with homesteaded properties built before 2008 may qualify for grants. The program offers up to **$10,000** for specific hurricane hardening improvements. Check eligibility before signing a contract to maximize your savings.

## How to Save Money on Hurricane Shutter Installation in Boca Raton

1. **Bundle all openings in one project.** Installing shutters on 10 windows at once is cheaper per unit than doing them in two separate batches. Contractors save on setup and travel time, passing some savings to you.

2. **Choose accordion shutters for large openings.** If you have large sliding glass doors, accordion shutters are far cheaper than motorized roll-downs. They provide the same protection for a fraction of the price.

3. **Schedule during the off-season.** Avoid the peak hurricane prep months of August and September. Contractors are busier and prices are higher. Winter installations often come with **5–10%** discounts.

4. **Verify HOA approval early.** If you choose a style that your HOA later rejects, you will pay to remove and replace it. Confirming the aesthetic and technical requirements upfront prevents costly change orders.

5. **Ask about the My Safe Florida Home grant.** If you qualify, this program can cover a significant portion of your shutter costs. Do not sign a contract until you confirm your grant eligibility status.

## When to Install Hurricane Shutters — Warning Signs

**You have an aging roof or no shutters.** If your home lacks protection, a single storm can cause catastrophic damage. The cost of replacing windows and interior water damage far exceeds the cost of shutters.

**Your insurance premium is rising.** Wind mitigation discounts are disappearing for unprotected homes. Installing shutters can stabilize your premium and protect your insurability in a volatile market.

**You live near the coast.** Boca Raton homes within a mile of the beach face higher wind speeds and salt corrosion. Standard shutters may not suffice; you need high-grade HVHZ products.

**You have elderly or disabled residents.** Manual shutters can be difficult to deploy quickly during a sudden storm. Motorized roll-down shutters offer safety and ease of use for those who need it most.

**You are selling your home.** A home with impact-rated openings or shutters is more attractive to buyers. It reduces their insurance costs and fear of storm damage, potentially increasing your home's market value.

## How to Hire a Hurricane Shutter Contractor in Boca Raton

1. **Verify Florida license.** Check the contractor's status at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified or Registered Roofing Contractor or a General Contractor license.

2. **Confirm insurance coverage.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability and workers compensation. Ensure the policy is active and covers the full scope of the work.

3. **Check for Palm Beach County registration.** The contractor must have a local business tax receipt. This ensures they are a legitimate local business and not a transient storm chaser.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates.** Each quote should detail the shutter type, brand, NOA number, square footage, and total cost. Compare line items to ensure you are looking at the same scope.

5. **Review warranties.** Look for warranties on both the product (often 20+ years) and the workmanship (usually 2–5 years). A reputable contractor will stand behind their installation.

6. **Ask about Florida Product Approval.** The contractor must provide the specific approval number for the shutter system. If they cannot provide this, do not hire them.

7. **Check reviews.** Look for patterns in Google and Yelp reviews. Pay attention to comments about punctuality, cleanup, and how they handled unexpected issues during installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install hurricane shutters in Boca Raton in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$15.00 and $45.00 per square foot**. A typical full-home installation ranges from **$12,000 to $35,000**. The price depends on the shutter type, the number of openings, and any custom requirements.

### Do I need a permit to install hurricane shutters in Palm Beach County?

Yes, always. You must pull a building permit through the Palm Beach County Development Services Center. The fee is typically **$200–$500**. The county requires an inspection to verify the wind-load rating and installation quality.

### How long does hurricane shutter installation take?

A standard installation for an average home takes **1 to 3 days**. Larger homes or those with complex custom windows may take up to **5 days**. Weather delays are rare for indoor shutter work but can happen if exterior access is blocked.

### Do hurricane shutters lower my insurance premium?

Yes, often. Installing approved shutters can qualify you for a wind mitigation discount. These discounts typically range from **15% to 25%** on your wind insurance portion. Contact your carrier for specific savings calculations.

### Can I install hurricane shutters myself in Florida?

Florida law allows homeowners to install shutters on their own primary residence. However, you still need a permit and must pass inspection. Insurance companies may deny coverage if the installation does not meet code standards.

### What is the difference between accordion and roll-down shutters?

Accordion shutters fold and are cheaper but remain visible when stowed. Roll-down shutters retract into a housing above the window, offering a cleaner look and higher security but at a higher cost.
