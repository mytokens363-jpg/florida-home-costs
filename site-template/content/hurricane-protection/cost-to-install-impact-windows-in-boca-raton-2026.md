# How Much Does It Cost to Install Impact Windows in Boca Raton? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing impact windows in Boca Raton costs between **$15,000 and $45,000** for a typical single-family home, with the average homeowner paying around **$27,000**. The price depends heavily on the number of windows, frame material choice, and Palm Beach County permit requirements.

Boca Raton sits directly on the Atlantic coast, where salt air and hurricane winds demand more than standard glass. Impact windows are not just a luxury here; they are often the only way to maintain insurance eligibility on coastal properties.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$15,000 – $22,000** | Vinyl frames, 10–12 windows |
| Mid-Range | **$22,000 – $32,000** | Aluminum frames, 15–20 windows |
| High-End | **$32,000 – $45,000+** | Steel frames, custom sizes, doors |

**Budget** installs usually feature vinyl frames with standard impact glass. This is a solid choice for older homes where budget is the primary driver. It meets Florida Building Code requirements for the Wind-Borne Debris Region.

**Mid-range** is the most common choice for Boca Raton homeowners. It typically involves aluminum or steel frames which resist salt corrosion better than vinyl. This range covers replacing all windows in a 2,000 square foot home with standard sizes.

**High-end** includes custom-sized windows, large sliding impact doors, and premium Low-E glass for energy savings. It often includes new frames that match the original architectural style of historic neighborhoods like Boca Grande.

These prices reflect Boca Raton market rates as of early 2026. They include materials, professional installation, disposal of old windows, standard permits, and cleanup. They do not include structural framing repairs if rot is discovered behind the existing windows.

## What Affects the Cost in Boca Raton

### 1. Number of Windows

The total price is calculated per window or per square foot of glazing. A standard 36x48 inch window costs between **$600 and $900** installed. A home with 20 windows will naturally cost more than one with 10. Large picture windows or sliding doors can run **$2,000 to $4,000** each.

### 2. Frame Material

Vinyl is the cheapest option at **$500–$800 per window** but can discolor under intense South Florida sun. Aluminum frames cost **$700–$1,100 per window** and resist salt air corrosion. Steel frames are the premium choice at **$900–$1,500 per window** but offer the slimmest profile for historic homes.

### 3. Installation Complexity

Replacing an existing window is straightforward. Replacing a window with a different size requires cutting new framing, which adds labor. Structural repairs to rotting wood around the frame can add **$500 to $2,000** to the job. High windows on two-story homes require scaffolding or lifts, adding **$300 to $600** per floor.

### 4. Palm Beach County Permits

Palm Beach County requires a building permit for window replacement if it involves structural changes or new openings. Permit fees range from **$200 to $600** depending on project valuation. The process includes a plan review and final inspection. Your contractor must submit product approval numbers (PBA) for the windows.

### 5. Insurance Requirements

Your insurance carrier may require specific impact ratings to qualify for wind mitigation credits. Standard impact glass is rated to resist **150 mph winds** with debris impact. If your current windows are not rated, you cannot claim the full credit. Upgrading to code-compliant windows can save **$300–$600 annually** on premiums.

### 6. HOA Restrictions

Many Boca Raton communities have strict design guidelines regarding window color, frame style, and grid patterns. Some HOAs require custom grilles or specific aluminum finishes. Getting approval before ordering materials is essential. Changing windows after installation can cost thousands to undo.

## Cost by Material Type

| Material | Cost per Window | Lifespan |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Vinyl | **$500 – $800** | 20–25 years |
| Aluminum | **$700 – $1,100** | 30–40 years |
| Steel | **$900 – $1,500** | 40–50 years |
| Sliding Door | **$2,000 – $4,000** | 30–40 years |

**Vinyl** is the budget leader. It provides good insulation and meets impact codes. However, it can expand and contract more than metal in Boca Raton's heat, potentially leading to seal failures sooner than expected.

**Aluminum** is the workhorse of Florida construction. It does not rust and handles the salt air well. It is stronger than vinyl, allowing for larger glass panes without bulky frames. Most impact window manufacturers in South Florida use aluminum.

**Steel** offers the highest strength-to-weight ratio. It allows for narrower frames, maximizing glass area. This is popular for Mediterranean-style homes in Boca Raton where aesthetics matter. It costs significantly more but lasts longer.

**Sliding Doors** are the most expensive single units. They require heavy-duty tracks and reinforced frames to handle the size and wind load. A large sliding patio door often costs as much as **5 to 6 standard windows**.

**Custom Glass** upgrades like Low-E coatings or laminated tints add cost but improve energy efficiency. These reduce cooling bills by blocking UV rays. The added cost is **$150–$300 per window** but pays off in energy savings.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) mandates impact-resistant glazing in the Wind-Borne Debris Region (WBD). Boca Raton falls under this requirement due to its coastal location. All windows must carry a Product Approval Notice (PBA) from the state. Your contractor must provide this documentation during inspection.

**Permits:** Apply through Palm Beach County's ePlan portal or at the Building Department in West Palm Beach. Turnaround is typically 10–15 business days. Budget **$200–$600** for the permit fee. Two inspections are required: rough-in and final. The final inspection verifies the PBA numbers match the installed units.

**Insurance Impact:** Installing impact windows is one of the best ways to lower your wind premium. Most carriers offer a wind mitigation credit for impact-rated openings. This can reduce your premium by **10–25%** depending on the carrier. Some insurers will not renew a policy on a home without impact windows if it is in a high-risk zone.

**My Safe Florida Home:** This state program offers grants and rebates for hurricane hardening. Homeowners can receive up to **$10,000** in matching grants for impact window upgrades. The program requires a pre-inspection to determine eligibility. You must apply before the work begins to qualify for the rebate.

**HOA Approval:** Boca Raton has many deed-restricted communities. The Design Review Committee (DRC) must approve window changes. They often require samples or photos of the proposed window. Failing to get approval can result in fines or being forced to remove the windows.

**Season:** Best installation time is December through April. Dry weather ensures proper sealing and curing of adhesives. Avoid installing during peak hurricane season (August–October). Rain delays can compromise the weather barrier if the house is open too long.

**Salt Air:** Coastal air corrodes metal frames faster than inland areas. Aluminum must be anodized or powder-coated specifically for coastal environments. Vinyl frames in Boca Raton should be UV-resistant to prevent yellowing over time.

## How to Save Money on Impact Window Installation in Boca Raton

1.  **Apply for MSFH rebates early.** The My Safe Florida Home program has limited funding. Submit your application and inspection request before signing a contract. This can cover **10–20%** of the project cost if you qualify.

2.  **Bundle the project.** Replace all windows at once rather than in phases. Contractors offer volume discounts on labor and materials. Splitting the job into two parts can cost **10% more** due to mobilization fees and setup costs.

3.  **Choose aluminum over steel.** Aluminum provides the same impact protection as steel for a fraction of the cost. Unless you need the ultra-slim profile of steel, aluminum is the smarter financial choice for 90% of homeowners.

4.  **Schedule in the dry season.** Installers are less busy from December to April. You may negotiate a better price or get priority scheduling. Rain delays in summer can extend the timeline and increase labor costs.

5.  **Compare per-window pricing.** Some contractors quote per square foot, others per window. Get quotes based on the same number of units to compare apples to apples. A difference of **$100 per window** on 20 windows equals **$2,000**.

## When to Install Impact Windows — Warning Signs

**Your insurance premium is rising.** If your wind mitigation credits are expiring or your premium is doubling, impact windows are the fastest fix. A new roof or impact windows can reset your mitigation status with the carrier.

**You hear wind whistling.** Drafts around the window frame indicate the seal has failed. In Boca Raton, wind-driven rain can enter through gaps and cause interior mold. Replacing windows is cheaper than repairing water damage later.

**Glass is scratched or pitted.** Coastal salt air pitting glass makes it look cloudy. While this doesn't affect safety, it reduces visibility and home value. If the glass is damaged, the impact rating is compromised.

**You live in a flood zone.** If your home is in an AE or VE flood zone, impact windows often serve as the required barrier for lower floors. Local code may mandate impact glazing in these specific areas.

**Windows are 15+ years old.** Standard windows typically last 15–20 years. Impact windows last longer. If your windows are nearing the end of their life, upgrading to impact during replacement saves double the cost of doing it twice.

## How to Hire a Window Contractor in Boca Raton

1.  **Verify their Florida license.** Search for a Certified Window Contractor (CWC) or General Contractor (CGC) at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). Impact window installation requires specific competency in sealing and anchoring.

2.  **Confirm product approvals.** Ask for the Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval number for the specific window model. If they cannot provide it, the windows may not meet code.

3.  **Check for local experience.** Boca Raton has specific wind zones and HOA rules. A contractor from Orlando may not know the local permit nuances. Ask how many impact window jobs they have completed in Palm Beach County this year.

4.  **Get a detailed contract.** The contract must list every window size, glass type, frame material, and warranty terms. It should also state who pulls the permit and handles the final inspection.

5.  **Review the warranty.** Look for a warranty that covers both the glass and the frame. Many manufacturers offer 10-year warranties, but some premium brands offer lifetime coverage. Ensure the warranty is transferable if you sell the home.

6.  **Avoid storm chasers.** Be wary of contractors who knock on doors after a storm. They may not be licensed in Florida. Verify their license number and physical address in Palm Beach County.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a single impact window cost in Boca Raton?

A standard 36x48 inch impact window costs between **$600 and $900** installed. This includes the frame, glass, and labor. Custom sizes or sliding doors cost more, ranging from **$1,500 to $4,000** per unit.

### Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Palm Beach County?

Yes, usually. If you are replacing existing openings, a permit is often required to verify the product meets code. If you are changing the opening size, a permit is mandatory. Budget **$200–$600** for the permit fee.

### Can I get a rebate for impact windows in Florida?

Yes, the My Safe Florida Home program offers rebates up to **$10,000**. You must pass a wind mitigation inspection first. The rebate is a matching grant for eligible improvements.

### How long does the installation take?

Installing 15–20 windows typically takes **2 to 4 days**. Large sliding doors may add an extra day. Weather can delay the process if rain is forecasted during the tear-out phase.

### Do impact windows improve energy efficiency?

Yes, impact glass often includes Low-E coatings that block heat. This can lower cooling costs by **10–20%** during Florida summers. The tight seal also reduces air leakage around the frame.

### Will my insurance premium drop?

Most carriers offer wind mitigation credits for impact windows. This can reduce your premium by **10–25%**. The savings usually offset the installation cost over 10 to 15 years.
