# How Much Does It Cost to Install Impact Windows in Weston? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** A full home impact window installation in Weston costs between **$25,000 and $60,000** for most single-family homes, with the average homeowner paying around **$38,000**. The biggest factors driving your price are window quantity, frame material, and Broward County's strict wind mitigation building requirements.

Weston sits in Broward County, which enforces some of the toughest window standards in the state. Even though Weston is inland, the wind zone requirements are nearly identical to coastal areas. That adds cost — but it also means your home is protected against the severe storms that hit South Florida every year.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$25,000 – $35,000** | Vinyl frames, 10–15 windows |
| Mid-Range | **$35,000 – $50,000** | Aluminum, 15–25 windows |
| High-End | **$50,000 – $75,000+** | Wood/Fiberglass, 25+ windows |

**Budget** gets you standard vinyl frames with dual-pane impact glass on a smaller home. It meets Florida Building Code but uses entry-level glass coatings.

**Mid-range** is what most Weston homeowners choose — aluminum frames with thermal breaks, low-E glass, and 25–30 windows. Covers homes in the 2,000 to 3,000 sq ft range.

**High-end** covers wood or fiberglass frames on larger estates. Includes enhanced soundproofing glass, custom finishes, and premium hardware.

These prices reflect Weston market rates as of early 2026 and include materials, labor, tear-out and disposal, standard permits, and cleanup. They do not include structural repairs if damage is found during the window removal process.

## What Affects the Cost in Weston

### 1. Window Count and Size

Windows are priced by the opening, not just the square footage. A standard 3x5 window costs less than a custom 6x6 picture window. A typical Weston home has between 20 and 30 windows. Small homes with fewer openings start near **$25,000**, while large estates with 40+ windows can exceed **$75,000**.

### 2. Frame Material

This is the single biggest price lever you control. Vinyl frames are the most common option at **$600–$900 per window** installed. Aluminum frames — popular in Florida for durability — jump to **$900–$1,500**. Wood frames are the premium choice at **$1,500–$3,000 per window** but require more maintenance.

### 3. Glass Specifications

Standard impact glass has two panes with a vinyl interlayer. Upgrades include Low-E coating for heat reduction, argon gas fill for insulation, and tinted glass for UV protection. Upgrading from standard to premium glass adds **$150–$400 per window**. This is critical for Weston's intense summer heat.

### 4. Labor Rates in Broward County

Window installation labor in Broward County runs **$60–$90 per hour**, roughly 15–20% above the national average. Demand stays high year-round from storm damage repairs and insurance-mandated upgrades. After a named storm, rates can spike another 10–20% due to backlog.

### 5. Permits and Inspections

Broward County requires a building permit for any window replacement that involves structural changes. Permit fees run **$400–$900** depending on project value. The process includes a plan review and final inspection. Your contractor should pull the permit — if they suggest skipping it, walk away.

### 6. Structural Modifications

If your home was built before 2000, the existing framing might not meet current code. You may need to install new headers or reinforce the rough opening. Structural work can add **$2,000–$8,000** to the project depending on how much framing needs repair.

| Material | Cost per Window | Lifespan |

**Vinyl impact** is the budget option. It resists rot and requires no painting. Best for standard homes and budget-conscious homeowners.

**Aluminum impact** hits the sweet spot — rated to 180 mph, 30-year lifespan, and the best cost-to-value ratio. This is what most Weston homeowners pick.

**Fiberglass** is durable and expands with temperature changes. Rated to 180 mph and lasts 30–50 years but costs roughly double vinyl. Your contractor needs special tools for installation.

**Wood impact** is premium — 30+ year lifespan, 180 mph rating, and the aesthetic that fits high-end Weston neighborhoods. Expect to pay **$1,500–$3,000 per window**.

**Glass upgrades** (Low-E, tinted) add to the base price but reduce cooling costs. In Weston's climate, this saves money on AC bills over time.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) governs all window replacements in Weston. Key requirements include impact-resistant glazing tested to missile impact and cyclic pressure tests. These go beyond what most states require for standard windows.

**Hurricane Zone:** Weston is in Wind Zone 3. All window products must carry a Florida Product Approval (FPA). Products are tested with missile impact tests and cyclic pressure tests that simulate hurricane conditions. Your contractor should provide FPA numbers for every product they plan to use.

**Permits:** Apply through Broward County's ePlan online portal or at the Broward County Building Department. Turnaround is 5–10 business days for a standard residential permit. Budget **$400–$900** for the fee. One inspection required: final.

**Insurance Impact:** This is the big one. A new impact window system resets your wind mitigation profile. Many Florida insurers offer discounts of **10–25%** on your premium for impact-rated openings. Upgrading from standard to impact can save hundreds per year.

**HOA:** Many Weston communities restrict window style, color, and hardware finish. Some require matching the original architectural style. Get written HOA approval before signing a contractor agreement — changing materials mid-job is extremely expensive.

**Season:** Best time to install windows here is December through April — dry season, lower humidity, fewer storm damage backlogs. Avoid peak hurricane season (August–October). Rainy season (May–October) brings daily afternoon thunderstorms that delay projects.

**My Safe Florida Home:** If your home was built before 2008 and is homesteaded, you may qualify for a free wind inspection and matching grants up to **$10,000** toward hurricane hardening improvements including window upgrades.

## How to Save Money on Impact Windows in Weston

1. **Get at least 4 written quotes.** Contractor pricing varies by 20–35% for identical scope in Weston. On a **$38,000** job, that's **$7,500–$13,000** in potential savings. Make sure each quote itemizes materials, labor, tear-off, permits, and disposal separately.

2. **Schedule in dry season.** December through April is slower for window installers. You'll have more leverage to negotiate and fewer weather delays. Some contractors offer **5–10%** off-season discounts.

3. **Keep existing openings if possible.** Replacing glass in existing frames is cheaper than full frame replacement. If frames are solid, ask about glass-only upgrades. The savings can be **$300–$500 per window**.

4. **Bundle hurricane upgrades with windows.** Adding storm shutters or reinforcing garage doors during a replacement is **40–60% cheaper** than standalone projects. The combined wind mitigation improvements can also cut your insurance premium by hundreds per year.

5. **Check for My Safe Florida Home eligibility.** If you qualify, the grant can cover a significant portion of the cost. Apply for the inspection first to determine your eligibility before signing a contractor agreement.

## When to Install Your Impact Windows — Warning Signs

**Your windows are 15+ years old.** Vinyl degrades faster in South Florida due to intense UV and heat cycling. A 15-year-old window in Weston has taken more abuse than a 20-year-old window in the Midwest.

**Your insurance company is pressuring you.** A non-renewal notice or letter requesting a window inspection means the clock is ticking. Many Florida insurers are actively dropping homes with non-impact windows in high-risk zones.

**Visible cracks or seal failure.** Check the glass for fogging or condensation between panes. This means the seal has failed and the insulating gas has escaped. It reduces energy efficiency significantly.

**Drafts and noise.** Feel for air leaks around the frame. In Weston's humid climate, leaks lead to moisture intrusion and mold fast. Multiple leaks usually mean the whole system has failed.

## How to Hire a Window Contractor in Weston

1. **Verify their Florida license** at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified Windows and Glass Contractor (CGC) or Certified General Contractor (CGC) license.

2. **Confirm active insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum $1 million) and workers comp. Call the insurer directly to verify.

3. **Check for a Broward County local business tax receipt.** This confirms they're registered locally, not a storm chaser from out of state.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates** with line-item breakdowns — material brand, glass specs, number of windows, tear-off, permits, labor, timeline, and warranty.

5. **Check reviews on Google and BBB.** Look for patterns in complaints, not just star ratings.

6. **Never pay more than 10% upfront.** Florida Statute 489.126 restricts contractor deposits. Payment should tie to milestones: deposit, tear-off complete, mid-project, final inspection passed.

7. **Get everything in writing.** Contract must include: scope, material specs with FPA numbers, dates, payment schedule, warranty terms, permit responsibility, and cleanup plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install impact windows in Weston in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$25,000 and $60,000**. The average is around **$38,000**. Your actual cost depends on window count, material choice, complexity, and whether structural repairs are needed.

### Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Broward County?

Yes, always. Apply through Broward County's ePlan portal or at the Building Department. Budget **$400–$900**. One inspection required. Never let a contractor skip the permit — it can void your insurance and create title issues when you sell.

### How long does an impact window installation take in Weston?

Vinyl windows take **3–5 days** for an average home. Aluminum or wood windows take **5–10 days**. Rainy season can add 2–5 days of weather delays.

### Do impact windows increase home value in Weston?

Impact windows recoup **70–80%** of their cost at resale. But the bigger value in Weston's market is insurability — a home with impact windows is dramatically easier to sell because buyers can actually get insurance on it.

### Can I install impact windows myself in Florida?

Florida's homeowner exemption allows it if you own and occupy the home. But you still need permits, inspections, and must meet all Florida Building Code requirements. Insurance companies may refuse to cover a homeowner-installed window system. For most people, it's not worth the risk.

### Does homeowners insurance cover window replacement?

Storm damage (wind, hail, fallen trees) is typically covered minus your deductible — often **2% of insured value** for hurricane claims in Florida. Normal wear and aging is never covered. Impact windows lower your deductible for wind claims in some policies.
