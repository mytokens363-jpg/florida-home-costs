# How Much Does It Cost to Install Impact Windows in Pembroke Pines? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing impact windows in Pembroke Pines costs between **$15,000 and $45,000** for a typical single-family home, with the average project landing around **$28,000**. The final price depends heavily on window count, frame material, and whether you are doing a full home retrofit or a partial upgrade.

Pembroke Pines homes face significant wind risks from tropical storms and hurricanes. Standard windows shatter easily under pressure, but impact-rated glass stays intact. This protection saves your home from water damage and wind pressure breaches that can lead to total roof failure.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$15,000 – $22,000** | Vinyl frames, 10–15 windows |
| Mid-Range | **$22,000 – $32,000** | Aluminum frames, 15–20 windows |
| High-End | **$32,000 – $45,000+** | Custom sizes, 20+ windows |

**Budget** projects typically involve standard vinyl frames with pre-sized impact glass. These fit most existing openings without major structural changes. Expect **$600–$900 per window** installed.

**Mid-range** upgrades usually involve aluminum frames which offer better durability and slimmer profiles. This tier often includes 15 to 20 windows and might include some structural reinforcement. Prices average **$900–$1,300 per window**.

**High-end** projects cover custom shapes, larger sliding doors, or premium finishes like wood-clad aluminum. These require specialized labor and often involve replacing the entire window frame and sill. Costs rise to **$1,300–$2,000+ per unit**.

These figures cover materials, installation labor, disposal of old windows, and standard Broward County permitting fees. They assume no structural framing repairs are needed behind the existing walls.

## What Affects the Cost in Pembroke Pines

### 1. Window Count and Size

Windows are priced individually, but volume discounts apply. A typical Pembroke Pines home has 12 to 20 operable windows plus patio doors. Standard single-hung windows cost less than large picture windows or sliding glass doors. Each additional opening adds **$500–$1,500** to the total bill.

### 2. Frame Material Choice

Vinyl is the most affordable option, offering good insulation and no painting requirements. Aluminum frames cost more but are stronger and resist warping in heat. Wood-clad aluminum is the premium choice for aesthetics. Expect vinyl to run **$600–$900 per window** while aluminum starts at **$900–$1,400**.

### 3. Labor Rates in Broward County

Skilled window installers in South Florida charge **$60–$90 per hour** for installation labor. This rate is higher than the national average due to demand and the specialized skills needed for impact-rated units. Specialized crews often charge flat rates per window rather than hourly.

### 4. Permits and Inspections

Pembroke Pines requires a building permit for window replacements that involve structural changes or new openings. The City of Pembroke Pines Building Department handles these. Permit fees typically range from **$300 to $800** depending on the number of openings and project valuation.

### 5. Retrofit vs. New Construction

Retrofitting existing openings is cheaper because the rough opening already exists. New construction or adding new windows requires cutting into stucco and block walls. Adding new openings can add **$1,000–$3,000** per window due to framing and stucco repair work.

### 6. Florida Building Code

Pembroke Pines falls under Wind Zone II requirements, requiring specific wind load ratings. While not HVHZ, products must still pass Florida Product Approval. This mandates specific testing and certification numbers on every window unit. Compliance adds **$100–$300** per window to material costs compared to non-compliant imports.

## Cost by Material Type

| Material | Cost per Window | Lifespan |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Vinyl | **$600 – $900** | 20–25 years |
| Aluminum | **$900 – $1,400** | 30–40 years |
| Fiberglass | **$1,000 – $1,600** | 30–50 years |
| Impact Glass Only | **$400 – $700** | 20+ years |
| Triple-Pane | **$1,400 – $2,000** | 25–30 years |

**Vinyl** frames are the most popular choice for Pembroke Pines homeowners. They resist corrosion from salt air and require minimal maintenance. The trade-off is they cannot be painted and may expand in extreme heat.

**Aluminum** frames offer superior strength and a sleek, modern look. They are often used in high-rise condos or modern Florida homes. They conduct heat more than vinyl, so thermal breaks are essential for energy efficiency.

**Fiberglass** frames are incredibly strong and stable. They expand and contract less than vinyl or aluminum in Florida's heat. This material is less common but offers better longevity for harsh coastal environments.

**Impact Glass Only** applies if you keep your existing frames. This is rare in Pembroke Pines because old frames often fail before the glass does. It is only viable if your current frames are in perfect condition.

**Triple-Pane** units provide maximum sound and heat insulation. They are heavier and require stronger hardware. The cost premium is significant, often doubling the price of standard impact windows.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition governs all window work in Pembroke Pines. You must comply with wind pressure ratings specific to your zone. New openings require structural headers to support the load above the window.

**Wind Zone:** Pembroke Pines is in Wind Zone II, which requires windows to withstand 130 mph winds. This is less strict than Miami-Dade HVHZ, but still requires certified impact products. Non-certified windows will fail inspection and void insurance coverage.

**Permits:** Submit plans through the City of Pembroke Pines Building Department online portal. The review process typically takes 7–14 business days. You must schedule a final inspection once installation is complete.

**Insurance Impact:** Installing impact windows can reduce your windstorm deductible and premium. Some carriers offer discounts of **15–30%** on the wind portion of your policy. Always get a wind mitigation inspection after installation to claim the credit.

**HOA:** Many Pembroke Pines neighborhoods have Homeowners Associations with strict appearance rules. You may need approval for frame color, grid patterns, or visible hardware. Check your CC&Rs before ordering materials to avoid costly replacements.

**Season:** Schedule installations during the dry season, November through April. Rain delays are common in the summer and can stall projects. High heat in July and August can make working on window frames difficult for installers.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners in Pembroke Pines may qualify for grants up to **$10,000** for window upgrades. This program requires a pre-inspection and matching funds from the homeowner. The grant covers hardening improvements to reduce storm risk.

1. **Get at least 5 written quotes.** Contractor pricing varies by 30% for identical scope in Broward County. On a **$25,000** job, that's **$7,500** in potential savings. Ensure each quote includes permit fees and disposal costs.

2. **Bundle with other hardening projects.** Combining window upgrades with door replacements or roof tie-downs reduces mobilization costs. Contractors often offer **10–15%** off labor when bundling multiple exterior improvements.

3. **Choose standard sizes.** Custom window sizes cost significantly more. Stick to standard rough openings if possible. If your home has non-standard openings, consider replacing the frame to fit standard stock sizes.

4. **Check for My Safe Florida Home rebates.** This state program offers matching grants for wind mitigation. Visit their website to verify eligibility before signing a contract. The rebate can offset **20–30%** of the project cost.

5. **Schedule in the off-season.** Window installers are busier in spring and fall. Winter months often see slower demand. Ask contractors if they offer discounts for scheduling work between December and February.

## When to Replace Your Windows — Warning Signs

**Your windows are 15 years old.** Most impact windows come with 10–20 year warranties. Older windows lose their seal and thermal efficiency. In Pembroke Pines, UV exposure degrades frames faster than in cooler climates.

**You feel drafts or hear noise.** Air leakage indicates the weatherstripping has failed. If you hear wind noise during storms, the glass is not sealing properly. This compromises your home's pressure barrier during hurricanes.

**Visible water damage or rot.** Check the interior frame for soft wood or peeling paint. Water intrusion often starts at the sill. Rot compromises structural integrity and requires more extensive repairs than simple window replacement.

**Insurance is threatening renewal.** If your carrier sends a non-renewal notice citing window deficiencies, act immediately. Some policies require impact-rated windows in certain zones for coverage.

A full home installation takes **3–7 days**. Larger homes with 20+ windows may take **10 days**. Weather delays in summer can extend this timeline by a few days.

### Do impact windows increase home value in Pembroke Pines?

### Can I replace my own windows in Florida?

Homeowners can perform this work if they own and occupy the home. You still need permits and inspections. Insurance companies may refuse coverage on DIY installations.

### Does homeowners insurance cover window replacement?

Storm damage (wind, hail) is typically covered minus your deductible. Normal wear and aging is never covered. Impact windows may lower your deductible on future claims.
