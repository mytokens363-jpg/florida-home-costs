# How Much Does It Cost to Install Impact Windows in Hollywood? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing impact-resistant windows in Hollywood costs between **$4,800 and $18,500** for a typical single-family home, with the average project landing around **$9,200**. The final price depends heavily on the number of openings, frame material, and the strict Miami-Dade County High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) requirements that apply to Broward County.

Hollywood sits right on the edge of the HVHZ, meaning your new windows must pass rigorous missile impact and cyclic pressure tests. This isn't just about glass; it's about a certified frame system that keeps your home pressurized during a Category 4 or 5 storm.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$4,800 – $6,500** | 10-12 vinyl sliders, basic glass |
| Mid-Range | **$6,500 – $11,000** | 15-20 units, aluminum/clad, upgrades |

**Budget** projects usually involve replacing a few sliding patio doors or standard bedroom windows with vinyl frames and single-pane impact glass. It covers a modest number of openings, typically 10 to 12 units.

**Mid-range** is the sweet spot for most Hollywood homeowners. This includes 15 to 20 windows, often mixing aluminum or aluminum-clad frames with dual-pane Low-E glass for better energy efficiency and noise reduction.

**High-end** covers entire home retrofits with 25 or more units. This tier often features wood-clad frames for a premium look, custom sizes, and specialized glass tints or laminates for maximum UV protection.

These figures reflect Hollywood market rates for early 2026 and include materials, labor, disposal of old windows, and standard Broward County permits. They do not include structural repairs to the rough opening if rot is discovered during removal.

## What Affects the Cost in Hollywood

### 1. Number of Openings

The single biggest driver of cost is simply how many windows you are replacing. A typical Hollywood home has between 15 and 25 window openings. Installing impact glass at **$300–$600 per opening** for basic vinyl can add up quickly. Replacing 20 units at the mid-range average of **$550 each** hits **$11,000**.

### 2. Frame Material Choice

Vinyl frames are the most affordable option, typically costing **$250–$450 per unit** installed. Aluminum frames cost slightly more at **$400–$700 per unit** but offer a slimmer profile and higher strength. Wood-clad frames are the premium choice, running **$800–$1,200 per unit** installed, offering superior aesthetics for historic Hollywood neighborhoods.

### 3. Glass Specifications

Standard single-pane impact glass is the baseline. Upgrading to dual-pane Low-E glass adds **$100–$200 per unit** but significantly improves energy efficiency and reduces heat gain during Florida's intense summer. Adding tinting or special security laminates can add another **$50–$150 per pane**.

### 4. Labor Rates in Broward County

Labor for window installation in Hollywood is higher than the national average due to high demand and strict code requirements. Expect to pay **$60–$90 per hour** for skilled installers. A full home replacement often takes 2 to 4 days, meaning labor costs can range from **$2,500 to $5,000** depending on the crew size and project complexity.

### 5. Permit Fees in Hollywood

Hollywood requires a building permit for all impact window replacements. The Broward County Building Department processes these applications, and fees are based on the total project value. You should budget **$150–$400** for the permit, which includes a plan review and a final inspection to verify HVHZ compliance.

### 6. HVHZ Compliance

Hollywood is part of the High Velocity Hurricane Zone. All windows must carry a Miami-Dade NOA (Notice of Acceptance) or a Florida Product Approval number. This certification ensures the unit has passed missile impact tests. Products without this certification cannot be installed legally and will fail inspection, forcing a costly re-do.

**Vinyl sliders** are the most common choice for Hollywood homes. They are affordable, low maintenance, and effective at blocking wind. They work well for bedrooms and living rooms where sliding operation is needed.

**Vinyl casement** windows offer better sealing against wind and water than sliders. They crank open for ventilation and are excellent for kitchens and bathrooms where moisture control is critical.

**Aluminum sliders** are popular for large patio doors and wide openings. They offer a sleek, modern look and superior structural strength. They are slightly more expensive but handle the wind loads of the coast better than standard vinyl.

**Aluminum casement** windows combine the strength of metal with the tight seal of a crank-out design. They are the top choice for homeowners in the most exposed coastal sections of Hollywood who need maximum wind resistance.

**Wood-clad** windows feature an interior wood finish for a classic look with an exterior aluminum or vinyl shell for durability. They are the most expensive option but add significant curb appeal to historic bungalows in Hollywood.

**Fixed panes** are non-opening windows used for light and views. They are the most affordable impact option and are often used in combination with operable windows to create a cohesive facade.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code (8th Edition, 2023) mandates that all windows in coastal zones meet specific wind pressure ratings. In Hollywood, this means your windows must be rated for the specific wind zone, often 150 mph or higher. The code also requires proper flashing and sealing to prevent water intrusion during heavy rain.

**Hurricane Zone:** Hollywood is in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ). This means every window unit must have a valid Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval. The glass must pass a large missile impact test and a cyclic pressure test. Without these documents, the window is illegal to install and will not pass inspection.

**Permits:** Broward County requires a permit for any window replacement. You must submit the product's NOA number, energy rating, and installation details. The process is handled through the Broward County ePlan portal. Expect a turnaround of 3–7 business days for approval, with a final inspection required after installation.

**Insurance Impact:** Installing impact windows is one of the most effective ways to lower your homeowners insurance premium in Florida. Many carriers offer discounts of **15–35%** on the wind mitigation portion of your policy. Some insurers may even offer a flat dollar discount of **$300–$800 per year** for a fully impact-rated home.

**HOA Restrictions:** Many Hollywood neighborhoods, especially those near the beach or in older developments, have strict Homeowners Association (HOA) rules. These rules often dictate the color, style, and visible hardware of your windows. Always get written approval from your HOA before ordering custom windows to avoid costly changes later.

**Season:** The best time to install windows is during the dry season, from **November to April**. This avoids the peak of hurricane season and the heavy afternoon thunderstorms of May through October. Rain delays can stall a project, so scheduling in the dry months ensures a faster completion.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners with homesteaded properties built before 2008 may qualify for the My Safe Florida Home program. This offers free wind inspections and grants of up to **$10,000** for hurricane hardening improvements, including impact window upgrades. Check eligibility before starting your project.

## How to Save Money on Impact Window Installation in Hollywood

1. **Bundle the entire home replacement.** Replacing all windows at once is cheaper per unit than doing them in phases. Contractors often offer a **10–15% discount** on the total labor cost for full-home projects. It also minimizes the number of permit applications and inspections needed.

2. **Stick to vinyl for interior rooms.** If your HOA allows it, use impact vinyl for bedrooms and living areas where aesthetics are less critical. Save the premium aluminum or wood-clad frames for the front of the house or large patio doors where they make the most visual impact.

3. **Check for insurance discounts first.** Before signing a contract, get a wind mitigation quote from your current insurer. If you are already eligible for a discount, ensure your contractor provides the necessary NOA documentation to process it immediately. This can offset **$500–$1,000** of your upfront cost.

4. **Schedule during the off-season.** Window contractors are busiest from August to October after storm season. Scheduling your project in **January or February** can give you more leverage to negotiate pricing. Some contractors may offer **5–10%** off to keep their crews busy during the slower winter months.

5. **Prepare the rough openings.** If you have old windows with rot or water damage, the contractor may need to repair the framing. Doing minor carpentry work yourself beforehand can save on labor hours. However, be careful not to compromise the structural integrity of the opening.

## When to Replace Your Windows — Warning Signs

**Your windows are 20+ years old.** Impact technology has advanced significantly. Older windows, even if they look okay, likely lack the proper certification for the current HVHZ code. They may fail inspection if you need to replace them later due to storm damage.

**You feel drafts or hear wind noise.** In Hollywood's high winds, old windows often develop gaps in the seals or frames. If you feel a draft or hear a whistling sound during a storm, your windows are no longer providing a secure barrier. This also increases your energy bills as your AC works harder.

**Condensation between panes.** If you see fog or moisture trapped between the glass layers, the seal has failed. This reduces the insulating value of the window and indicates the unit is near the end of its life. It's a clear sign you need a replacement soon.

**Visible damage or warping.** Look for cracks in the glass, warped frames, or difficulty opening and closing windows. Salt air in Hollywood can corrode metal frames and degrade vinyl over time. Any visible structural weakness means the window won't hold up in the next hurricane.

## How to Hire a Window Contractor in Hollywood

1. **Verify Florida license.** Check the contractor's license at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified or Registered window contractor, or a general contractor with a window specialty.

2. **Confirm HVHZ experience.** Ask specifically if they have installed Miami-Dade NOA certified windows in Hollywood before. This is a specialized skill set. General handymen often lack the training to install these systems correctly.

3. **Check insurance and bonding.** Ensure the contractor carries general liability insurance and workers comp. Ask for a Certificate of Insurance and verify it directly with the carrier. This protects you if a worker gets hurt or property is damaged.

4. **Get 3–4 detailed quotes.** Each quote should list the specific window brand, model number, NOA number, frame material, glass type, and total labor cost. Compare line items, not just the bottom line.

5. **Review the warranty.** Look for a manufacturer's warranty on the glass and frame (often 10–20 years) and a separate workmanship warranty from the installer (usually 1–5 years). A good contractor stands behind their installation.

6. **Never pay full upfront.** Florida law restricts deposits. Pay a reasonable deposit for materials, but tie the rest of the payments to milestones. The final payment should only be made after the final inspection passes and the permit is closed.

7. **Get everything in writing.** The contract must specify the exact window models, NOA numbers, installation timeline, cleanup responsibilities, and who is responsible for pulling permits. Vague contracts lead to disputes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install impact windows in Hollywood in 2026?

The average cost for a full home installation is between **$4,800 and $18,500**, with most homeowners paying around **$9,200**. The price per window ranges from **$350 to $1,400** depending on the frame material and glass specifications.

### Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Hollywood?

Yes, Broward County requires a building permit for all window replacements. The permit fee typically ranges from **$150 to $400**. You must submit the product's NOA number, and a final inspection is required to verify the installation meets code.

### How long does an impact window installation take?

A typical Hollywood home with 15–20 windows takes **2 to 4 days** to install. Larger homes or custom orders with wood frames may take **5 to 7 days**. Weather delays during the rainy season can extend the timeline by a few days.

### Do impact windows increase my home's insurance premium?

No, they typically lower it. Installing impact windows often qualifies you for a wind mitigation discount of **15–35%** on your premium. Some carriers offer flat discounts of **$300–$800 annually** for fully impact-rated homes.

### Can I replace my own windows in Florida?

Florida's homeowner exemption allows you to do the work if you own and occupy the home. However, you must still pull permits, pass inspections, and ensure the windows meet HVHZ code. Most insurance companies will not honor claims for DIY installations that lack proper certification.

### What is the difference between "impact" and "storm" windows?

Impact windows are a complete system (frame and glass) designed to withstand hurricane-force winds and debris. Storm windows are secondary panels added to existing windows. Only full impact windows count toward insurance discounts and meet the strict HVHZ code requirements.
