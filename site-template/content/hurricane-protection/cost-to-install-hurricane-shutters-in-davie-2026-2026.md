# How Much Does It Cost to Install Hurricane Shutters in Davie? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing hurricane shutters in Davie costs between **$4,500 and $18,000** for a typical 3-bedroom home. The average homeowner pays around **$8,500** for a mix of roll-up and accordion styles. Your final price depends heavily on the shutter type, number of windows, and whether your home is in a High Velocity Hurricane Zone.

Davie sits squarely in Broward County, which enforces strict building codes for wind protection. While the city itself doesn't have a separate "HVHZ" designation like Miami-Dade, Broward County requires specific wind-load ratings for all storm protection products. This means you can't just buy cheap hardware store panels; you need certified systems that meet Florida Building Code standards.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$4,500 – $7,000** | Aluminum panels, 10–12 windows |
| Mid-Range | **$7,000 – $11,500** | Accordion or rolling shutters |
| High-End | **$11,500 – $18,000+** | Motorized rolling, full home coverage |

**Budget** covers removable aluminum panels that you store in a garage and screw in during storm season. These are the cheapest upfront but require physical labor every time a storm approaches. They typically cover 10 to 12 standard windows and doors.

**Mid-range** includes semi-permanent options like accordion shutters or manual rolling shutters. These are installed on the exterior and fold or roll away when not in use. They offer a better balance of convenience and cost for most Davie homeowners.

**High-end** features fully motorized rolling steel shutters or impact-rated windows with built-in protection. These systems operate via remote control or smart home integration. They provide the highest convenience and often come with the longest manufacturer warranties.

These figures represent current Davie market rates for early 2026. They include professional measurement, hardware installation, and the necessary permits. They do not cover structural modifications to the home's exterior if the current framing cannot support the new units.

## What Affects the Cost in Davie

### 1. Shutter Type Selection

The style of shutter is the primary driver of your total cost. Aluminum storm panels are the least expensive at **$15–$25 per sq ft** but require manual installation. Accordion shutters cost **$20–$35 per sq ft** and are semi-permanent. Rolling shutters range from **$30–$55 per sq ft** for manual units and **$45–$85 per sq ft** for motorized versions.

### 2. Window and Door Count

Davie homes vary significantly in size. A standard 1,800 sq ft home might have 14 windows and 2 garage doors. A larger 3,500 sq ft home could have 28 windows and 3 garage doors. Your contractor will quote based on the total square footage of the openings. More openings mean more tracks, more motors, and more labor hours.

### 3. Material and Gauge

Not all metal is created equal. Budget systems often use thinner aluminum that dents easily. High-quality systems use 24-gauge or 22-gauge steel or aluminum. Thicker materials cost more but resist impact better. In a storm, a thin panel might fail, while a heavy-gauge system holds firm.

### 4. Labor and Installation Complexity

Davie has many older homes with unique architectural details. Installing shutters on a home with curved bay windows or historic brickwork takes longer than a standard box window. Complex installations can add **$500–$1,500** to the labor bill. Motorized systems also require electrical work, which adds **$300–$800** per unit for wiring and outlets.

### 5. Broward County Permits

Broward County requires a building permit for installing permanent hurricane protection like roll-up or accordion shutters. Permit fees are calculated based on the project value, typically ranging from **$150 to $400**. The process involves submitting product specifications and engineering drawings to the county. Your contractor usually handles this, but the cost is passed to you.

### 6. Wind Load Requirements

Broward County is subject to specific wind load calculations based on your home's location relative to the coast. Homes closer to the Intracoastal or ocean require higher wind ratings. If your home is in a high-wind zone, you may need reinforced tracks or heavier gauge materials, adding **$1,000–$2,500** to the project.

## Cost by Shutter Type

| Shutter Type | Cost per Sq Ft | Installation Time |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Aluminum Panels | **$15 – $25** | 2–4 days |
| Accordion | **$20 – $35** | 1–3 days |
| Manual Roll-Up | **$30 – $55** | 2–5 days |
| Motorized Roll-Up | **$45 – $85** | 3–7 days |
| Impact Windows | **$50 – $120** | 5–10 days |

**Aluminum panels** are the entry-level choice. They are lightweight and easy to store but require you to physically remove and reinstall them before every storm. You need to keep a drill and bolts handy. They are the most budget-friendly option for cash-strapped homeowners.

**Accordion shutters** fold back against the house like an accordion. They are permanent fixtures that open and close quickly. They offer good protection and a clean look but can obstruct the view when closed. They are popular for large sliding glass doors.

**Manual roll-up shutters** are steel or aluminum rolls that you crank up and down. They provide excellent protection and don't obstruct the view when open. However, they require physical effort to operate, which can be difficult for elderly homeowners or those with mobility issues.

**Motorized roll-up shutters** are the premium convenience choice. A simple button press on a remote or wall switch opens and closes the shutters. They are ideal for large homes with many windows. The automation adds significant cost but saves time and effort during storm season.

**Impact windows** are a different category but often compared to shutters. They are windows with laminated glass that resist impact. They are the most expensive option but offer the best aesthetics and no storage needed. They function as both windows and shutters permanently.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) mandates that all hurricane protection products in Broward County must meet specific wind pressure ratings. Products must be tested and approved by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation. You cannot install uncertified products that fail to meet these standards.

**HVHZ and Broward:** While Davie is not in the Miami-Dade High Velocity Hurricane Zone, Broward County has its own strict wind codes. All shutters must be tested for missile impact and cyclic pressure. Your contractor must provide a Certificate of Compliance or a Miami-Dade NOA (Notice of Acceptance) for the products they install.

**Permits:** Broward County requires permits for permanent installations. You apply through the Broward County Building Department online portal or in person at the main office in Fort Lauderdale. The permit process takes 5–10 business days. Fees are typically **$150–$400** depending on the project scope.

**Insurance Impact:** Installing approved hurricane shutters can significantly lower your windstorm insurance premium. Many Florida carriers offer discounts of **5–20%** for homes with certified storm protection. Some carriers may even offer a flat dollar amount off your premium for having impact windows or shutters.

**HOA Restrictions:** Davie has many homeowner associations with strict architectural guidelines. Some HOAs prohibit visible exterior shutters or require specific colors and finishes. Always check your HOA covenants before purchasing. You may need HOA approval before the county issues a permit.

**Season:** The best time to install shutters is late winter or early spring (February to April). Contractors are less busy before hurricane season peaks in June. You can often negotiate better prices and faster scheduling during these months. Avoid trying to install during the peak rainy season when delays are common.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners in Davie may qualify for grants through the My Safe Florida Home program. This program offers up to **$10,000** in matching grants for hurricane hardening improvements, including shutter installation. You must have a free inspection first to determine eligibility.

## How to Save Money on Shutter Installation in Davie

1. **Get multiple quotes from local installers.** Pricing can vary by **30–40%** between companies in Broward County. A quote for **$10,000** at one shop might be **$7,000** at another. Ensure each quote includes the same product specifications and labor scope.

2. **Bundle window and door installations.** Installing shutters on all windows and doors at once is cheaper than doing them piecemeal. Contractors offer volume discounts for full-home projects. You save on travel time and setup costs.

3. **Choose manual over motorized for budget.** If you are handy, manual roll-up or accordion shutters save **$1,500–$3,000** compared to motorized versions. You only pay for the motor and electrical work if you need it.

4. **Ask about off-season discounts.** Contractors are often busiest in late summer. Scheduling your installation in the off-season (November to March) might get you a **5–10%** discount. Some companies offer "early bird" specials for spring bookings.

5. **Check for My Safe Florida Home rebates.** If you qualify, this program can cover a significant portion of your costs. The inspection is free, and the grant can be applied directly to the contractor. This is a legitimate way to reduce out-of-pocket expenses.

6. **Verify product warranties.** A cheap shutter that fails in a storm costs more in the long run. Look for products with **20+ year** warranties on the motor and finish. A longer warranty often indicates better quality materials and construction.

## When to Install Shutters — Warning Signs

**You live in a high-wind zone.** If your home is in Davie, you are subject to wind speeds of 150+ mph in severe storms. Without protection, windows are the most common point of failure during hurricanes. Broken windows can lead to roof damage and interior flooding.

**Your insurance premiums are rising.** If your windstorm deductible is high or your premium is increasing, adding shutters can help. Some carriers require shutters for homes over a certain age. Installing them now might prevent policy non-renewal in the future.

**You have old or damaged shutters.** If your current panels are bent, rusted, or missing, they are useless in a storm. Check your storage bin for rust or warping. If the brackets are loose or the panels don't fit, it's time to replace them.

**You are renovating or remodeling.** If you are replacing windows or siding, it is the perfect time to install new shutters. Coordinating the work saves labor costs and ensures the new system integrates perfectly with your home's exterior.

## How to Hire a Shutter Contractor in Davie

1. **Verify their license.** Check the contractor's license at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified or Registered Contractor with a valid license for storm protection or general contracting.

2. **Confirm insurance coverage.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability and workers' compensation. Florida law requires this for all licensed contractors. Call the insurer to verify the policy is active.

3. **Check for local experience.** Look for a company that installs shutters in Davie and Broward County regularly. They will know the specific permit requirements and common HOA restrictions in your neighborhood.

4. **Request product documentation.** Ask for the Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval number for the shutters. If they can't provide it, the product may not meet code. Do not accept "certified" without the official documentation.

5. **Get 3–4 written estimates.** Each quote should detail the product brand, gauge, motor type (if applicable), and installation timeline. Compare the line items, not just the total price.

6. **Review the contract carefully.** The contract must include the warranty terms, payment schedule, and permit responsibility. Never sign a contract that allows the contractor to change the product without your written approval.

7. **Avoid storm chasers.** Be wary of contractors who knock on doors after a storm. They often leave the area once the next storm hits. Stick with established local companies that have a physical address in Davie.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install hurricane shutters in Davie in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$4,500 and $18,000**. The average cost is around **$8,500** for a typical home. The price varies based on the shutter type and the number of openings.

### Do I need a permit to install shutters in Davie?

Yes, for permanent installations like roll-up or accordion shutters. You must apply through Broward County's Building Department. Permit fees typically range from **$150 to $400**. Removable panels usually do not require a permit.

### How long does installation take?

Most installations take **2 to 5 days** for an average home. Motorized systems may take longer due to electrical work. Complex homes with many windows can take up to a week.

### Do hurricane shutters increase home value in Davie?

They don't directly add resale value like a kitchen remodel, but they make the home more insurable. A home with certified storm protection is easier to sell in Florida because buyers know they can get insurance.

### Can I install shutters myself?

You can install removable panels yourself, but permanent systems usually require a licensed contractor for permits. DIY installation of permanent shutters may void your warranty and fail county inspections.

### Does homeowners insurance cover the cost of shutters?

Insurance generally does not pay for the installation of shutters unless they are damaged by a covered storm. However, installing them can lower your windstorm premium, saving you money over time.
