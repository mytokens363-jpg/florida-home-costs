# How Much Does It Cost to Install Impact Windows in Davie? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing impact windows in Davie typically costs between **$15,000 and $45,000** for a standard single-family home, with the average homeowner paying around **$28,000**. The final price depends heavily on the number of windows, frame material, and specific Broward County permit requirements.

Davie sits in Broward County, which has strict building codes for wind resistance even if it isn't in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) proper. These codes ensure your windows stop flying debris during a storm and keep your home pressurized.

This guide breaks down exactly what you pay for materials, labor, and permits in the 2026 market. We cover the real costs you face when upgrading your home's security and energy efficiency.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$15,000 – $22,000** | Vinyl frames, 10–15 windows |
| Mid-Range | **$22,000 – $35,000** | Aluminum frames, 15–25 windows |
| High-End | **$35,000 – $45,000+** | Custom sizes, wood cladding |

**Budget** projects usually involve standard vinyl frames with pre-sized openings. This covers replacing existing windows without structural changes to the rough opening. It works for homes built after 2002 that already have impact-ready walls.

**Mid-range** installations typically feature aluminum frames with better thermal breaks and larger glass panes. This scope includes 15 to 25 windows and often upgrades the frame trim to match newer architectural styles.

**High-end** covers custom shapes, wood-clad aluminum, or full-frame replacements that require new framing. These projects often include 25+ windows and premium hardware like multi-point locking systems.

These prices reflect Davie market rates as of early 2026. They include materials, labor, disposal of old windows, and standard permit fees. They do not include emergency tarping or storm damage repairs found during removal.

## What Affects the Cost in Davie

### 1. Number of Windows

The most obvious cost driver is simply how many windows you replace. Most Davie homes have between 15 and 30 windows. If you replace 10 windows, expect to pay **$1,200 to $1,800 per unit**. If you replace 30, the per-unit price drops slightly, but the total climbs to **$40,000+**.

### 2. Frame Material Choice

Vinyl frames are the most common choice at **$600–$1,000 per unit** installed. Aluminum frames cost more at **$1,000–$1,800 per unit** installed but offer better durability and a slimmer profile. Wood-clad aluminum is the premium option at **$1,500–$2,500 per unit**.

### 3. Glass Specifications

Impact glass is not just one type. Standard impact glass uses laminated safety glass with a PVB interlayer. Upgrading to Low-E glass or argon gas fills improves energy efficiency but adds **$150–$300 per window**. Tri-pane glass is available for high-efficiency needs but adds another **$400+ per unit**.

### 4. Labor Rates in Davie

Labor rates in Broward County are high due to demand and insurance regulations. Skilled window installers charge **$65–$95 per hour**. A standard 10-window replacement takes two crews about 2 days, adding **$2,500–$4,000** in labor alone. Specialized crews for high-rise or hard-to-reach areas charge more.

### 5. Permits and Inspections

Broward County requires a building permit for window replacement if the opening size changes or if it involves structural modifications. Permit fees run **$300–$800** depending on the project valuation. The process includes a plan review and a final inspection to verify the installation meets code.

### 6. Window Size and Complexity

Standard double-hung or slider windows are cheapest to install. Custom arched windows or large sliding glass doors cost significantly more due to fabrication time. A standard 4x4 window might cost **$1,500**, while a 6x8 sliding door runs **$3,500+** installed.

## Cost by Material Type

| Material | Cost per Unit | Lifespan |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Vinyl Impact | **$600 – $1,000** | 20–30 years |
| Aluminum Impact | **$1,000 – $1,800** | 30–40 years |
| Wood-Clad | **$1,500 – $2,500** | 40+ years |
| Steel Impact | **$2,000 – $3,500** | 50+ years |

**Vinyl impact** windows are the budget king. They offer good sound reduction and thermal performance. They are not as strong as metal but meet Florida wind codes for residential use. Best for cost-conscious upgrades.

**Aluminum impact** frames are the standard for durability. They resist warping and corrosion better than vinyl. They handle the intense Florida sun and humidity with less maintenance. Expect a 30-year life span with proper care.

**Wood-clad** windows offer the classic look of wood on the inside with aluminum on the outside. This combination satisfies HOA aesthetic requirements while protecting against rot. They cost more upfront but add significant curb appeal to Davie homes.

**Steel impact** windows are rare but extremely strong. They are often used on commercial buildings or high-end residential estates. The cost is high, but the security rating is unmatched. Best for properties in high-risk zones or with security concerns.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition governs window installations in Davie. All impact windows must pass ASTM E1886 and E1996 testing. This means they must withstand missile impact and cyclic pressure testing. Your contractor must provide a Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval number for every product.

**Wind Load:** Davie is not in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), but it is in a high-wind region. The design wind speed is typically 150 mph with gusts up to 170 mph. Windows must be rated for this specific pressure load based on your home's location and height.

**Permits:** Apply through the Broward County Building Department ePlan portal. You will need to submit product approval numbers and engineering drawings. Turnaround is 7–14 business days. Budget **$300–$800** for fees. Inspections occur after installation before final approval.

**Insurance Impact:** This is the biggest financial benefit. Installing impact windows can lower your wind insurance premium by **15–45%**. Many insurers offer "impact-resistant" credits. Some carriers may even refuse to insure homes without impact protection in certain zones.

**HOA:** Davie has many deed-restricted communities. Some HOAs restrict window color, grille patterns, or frame thickness. Aluminum frames are often preferred over vinyl in newer developments. Always get written approval before ordering custom windows.

**Season:** Installation is best done in the dry season, December through April. Rainy season (May–October) brings daily storms that can delay projects. If windows are removed, the house is vulnerable to rain damage during the work.

**My Safe Florida Home:** If your home is homesteaded, you may qualify for grants up to **$10,000** through this program. They offer free wind inspections and matching funds for hardening improvements. Check eligibility before signing a contract.

## How to Save Money on Window Installation in Davie

1. **Get at least 4 written quotes.** Contractor pricing varies by 20–30% for identical scope in Broward County. On a **$28,000** job, that is **$5,000–$8,000** in potential savings. Ensure each quote includes disposal fees and permit costs.

2. **Schedule in dry season.** December through April is slower for contractors. You will have more leverage to negotiate and fewer weather delays. Some contractors offer **5–10%** off-season discounts to keep crews working.

3. **Choose vinyl over aluminum if budget is tight.** The savings between vinyl (**$600–$1,000**) and aluminum (**$1,000–$1,800**) can be **$5,000–$10,000** on a whole-house project. Both meet code, but aluminum looks sleeker.

4. **Bundle with other upgrades.** Installing impact glass during a roof replacement or siding project saves on mobilization costs. You only pay the crew once to move equipment around the property. Combined projects can save **10–15%**.

5. **Check for insurance claim eligibility.** If existing windows are broken from a storm, your insurance may cover part of the replacement. File the claim before signing a contractor agreement. Be cautious of contractors who offer to "handle the insurance" — work with your adjuster directly.

## When to Replace Your Windows — Warning Signs

**Your windows are 20+ years old.** Most standard vinyl windows degrade in Florida heat. Seals fail, causing foggy glass. Frames warp, letting in air and water. A 20-year-old window in Davie has likely lost its structural integrity.

**You hear wind noise during storms.** If you feel drafts or hear whistling when the wind picks up, your windows are not sealed. Impact windows are rated to stop this noise and pressure. Old windows let the house pressurize, which can damage the roof.

**Your insurance premium is high.** High wind zones in Florida drive up premiums. Replacing standard windows with impact windows often triggers a discount. This can save **$1,000+ per year** on your premium, paying for the windows over time.

**Visible cracks or damage.** Check the frame for cracks or glass for chips. Even small cracks compromise the laminated safety layer. If the interlayer is compromised, the window might shatter in high winds.

## How to Hire a Window Contractor in Davie

1. **Verify their Florida license** at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified Roofing Contractor (CCC), Registered Roofing Contractor (RC), or General Contractor (CGC) license.

2. **Confirm active insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum $1 million) and workers comp. Call the insurer directly to verify.

3. **Check for a Broward County local business tax receipt.** This confirms they are registered locally, not a storm chaser from out of state.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates** with line-item breakdowns — material brand, glass type, number of units, tear-off, permits, labor, timeline, and warranty.

5. **Check reviews on Google and BBB.** Look for patterns in complaints, not just star ratings.

6. **Never pay more than 10% upfront.** Florida Statute 489.126 restricts contractor deposits. Payment should tie to milestones: deposit, delivery, installation, final inspection passed.

7. **Get everything in writing.** Contract must include: scope, material specs with NOA numbers, dates, payment schedule, warranty terms, permit responsibility, and cleanup plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install impact windows in Davie in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$15,000 and $45,000**. The average is around **$28,000**. Your actual cost depends on window count, material choice, and whether structural framing changes are needed.

### Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Broward County?

Yes, usually. Apply through Broward County's ePlan portal. Budget **$300–$800**. Inspections required. Never let a contractor skip the permit — it can void your insurance and create title issues when you sell.

### How long does impact window installation take in Davie?

A standard 10-window replacement takes **2–4 days**. Larger projects with 25+ windows take **5–10 days**. Rainy season can add 2–5 days of weather delays.

### Does replacing windows increase home value in Davie?

Impact windows recoup **70–80%** of their cost at resale. But the bigger value in Davie's market is insurability — a home with impact windows is dramatically easier to sell because buyers can actually get insurance on it.

### Can I install impact windows myself in Florida?

Florida's homeowner exemption allows it if you own and occupy the home. But you still need permits, inspections, and must meet all FBC code requirements. Insurance companies may refuse to cover a homeowner-installed window. For most people, it's not worth the risk.

### Does homeowners insurance cover window replacement?

Storm damage (wind, hail, fallen trees) is typically covered minus your deductible — often **2% of insured value** for hurricane claims in Florida. Normal wear and aging is never covered.
