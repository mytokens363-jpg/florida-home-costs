# How Much Does It Cost to Install Hurricane Shutters in Boca Raton? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing hurricane shutters in Boca Raton costs between **$8,000 and $25,000** for a typical single-family home, with the average homeowner paying around **$14,500**. The biggest factors driving your price are the number of openings, material selection, and whether you choose automated or manual systems.

Boca Raton sits in Palm Beach County, which carries strict wind load requirements similar to the rest of the state. Your home faces high winds from the Atlantic and the Intracoastal, so your shutters must meet Florida Building Code standards. This ensures they protect your home and keep your insurance premiums manageable.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$8,000 – $11,000** | Accordion, manual, 15 openings |
| Mid-Range | **$11,000 – $17,000** | Roll-down, manual, 25 openings |
| High-End | **$17,000 – $25,000+** | Automatic, motorized, 30+ openings |

**Budget** gets you manual accordion or colonial shutters. These are cost-effective and effective but require manual labor to close before a storm. They cover standard residential openings in the 15 to 20 range.

**Mid-range** often includes manual roll-down shutters or a mix of accordion and colonial styles. This is the most popular tier for homeowners who want reliability without the monthly subscription fees of full automation. Covers 20 to 30 openings.

**High-end** covers fully automatic motorized systems. You deploy them with a remote control or app. This includes the highest grade aluminum or steel and requires electrical work at every window. Covers 30 or more openings.

These prices reflect Boca Raton market rates as of early 2026 and include materials, labor, mounting hardware, and standard permits. They do not include structural reinforcement if your home requires it.

## What Affects the Cost in Boca Raton

### 1. Number of Openings

Shutter costs are often calculated per opening or per square foot of window area. A typical Boca Raton home has 20 to 30 windows plus garage doors. If you have 25 openings, a manual accordion system might cost **$12,000**. A full automatic system for the same home could reach **$22,000**.

### 2. Material Choice

Aluminum is the standard material for most shutters in South Florida. It resists salt air corrosion better than steel. Steel shutters are heavier and stronger but cost **15–20% more** due to material costs. Polycarbonate panels are lighter but scratch easier.

### 3. Labor Rates in Boca Raton

Palm Beach County labor rates are among the highest in the state. Skilled shutter installers charge **$65–$90 per hour**. This is higher than the national average due to demand from coastal properties. Storm season drives prices up, so scheduling in winter saves money.

### 4. Automation vs. Manual

Manual systems require you to physically lock them into place. This adds labor time during storm prep but saves on motor costs. Automatic systems add **$300–$500 per opening** for the motor and wiring. You also need an electrician to install the power source.

### 5. Permit Fees

Palm Beach County requires a building permit for permanent shutter installation. Fees run **$150–$400** depending on the project value. The county reviews the engineering stamp to ensure they meet wind load requirements.

### 6. Code Requirements

Boca Raton follows the Florida Building Code. All shutters must be tested for impact resistance or approved by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation. You cannot install non-rated shutters in many coastal zones.

## Cost by Material Type

| Material | Cost per Opening | Lifespan |
|---------|----------------|----------|
| Accordion | **$300 – $500** | 15–20 years |
| Roll-Down | **$500 – $800** | 20–25 years |
| Colonial | **$400 – $600** | 20–25 years |
| Bahama | **$600 – $900** | 20–30 years |
| Panel | **$250 – $400** | 15–20 years |
| Automatic | **$1,000+** | 20–25 years |

**Accordion shutters** are the most common budget option. They fold away into a side box when not in use. They are rated for high winds but require manual closing time.

**Roll-down shutters** are a popular mid-range choice. They slide down from a box above the window. They offer security benefits beyond just wind protection.

**Colonial shutters** are the traditional look. They fold in half against the wall. They look nice but can be heavy and difficult to operate for some homeowners.

**Bahama shutters** are hinged at the top and prop open. They are great for ventilation during normal weather. They provide a classic Caribbean aesthetic common in Boca.

**Panel shutters** are individual panels stored in a rack. You slide them into tracks during a storm. They are cheaper but require storage space and manual labor.

**Automatic systems** use motors to deploy any of the above styles. They are the premium choice for convenience and safety. You do not need to go outside to close them.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition governs all shutter installations in Boca Raton. You need a wind load rating based on your home's exposure category. Coastal homes face higher wind speeds than inland homes. Your contractor must submit engineering calculations with your permit application.

**Hurricane Zone:** Boca Raton is in a High Wind Zone. While not technically Miami-Dade HVHZ, Palm Beach County enforces strict wind standards. Products must carry Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA to be accepted. This ensures your shutters won't fail in 140 mph winds.

**Permits:** Apply through Palm Beach County Building and Environmental Management Department. The online portal allows you to upload engineering stamps and plans. Turnaround is 10–14 business days for review. Budget **$150–$400** for the permit fee. Inspections are required after installation.

**Insurance Impact:** This is critical. Installing approved shutters can lower your wind mitigation discount. Many carriers offer **15–25%** off your premium for impact-rated protection. Some insurers will not renew homes without approved storm protection in coastal zones.

**HOA:** Boca Raton has many deed-restricted communities. The Homeowners Association may dictate shutter color, style, and placement. You cannot install a motorized unit without HOA approval in some neighborhoods. Get written permission before buying materials.

**Season:** Hurricane season runs June through November. Install shutters between December and March. Contractors are less busy and can give you better pricing. Waiting until May often means long wait times for installation.

**My Safe Florida Home:** If your home is homesteaded and built before 2008, you may qualify for grants. The program offers up to **$10,000** for roof hardening and storm protection upgrades. Check eligibility at mysafefloridahome.com before signing a contract.

## How to Save Money on Hurricane Shutters

1. **Get at least 4 written quotes.** Contractor pricing varies by 20–30% in Palm Beach County. On a **$14,000** job, that is **$2,800–$4,200** in potential savings. Compare material brands and warranty terms carefully.

2. **Choose manual over automatic.** You save **$3,000–$5,000** by skipping motors. If you are physically able to close them, manual systems work just as well for protection.

3. **Bundle with roof work.** If you are replacing your roof, install shutters at the same time. Contractors often discount labor for multiple projects. It reduces the number of trips to your site.

4. **Time the installation.** Schedule for late winter or early spring. Avoid August and September when demand peaks. Some contractors offer **5–10%** off-season discounts to fill their schedule.

5. **Check insurance discounts first.** Ask your carrier what specific shutter types qualify for discounts. Some policies only accept certain NOA numbers. Installing the wrong type might not save you money on premiums.

## When to Install Shutters — Warning Signs

**Your current shutters are 15 years old.** Aluminum oxidizes and weakens over time. Check for cracks in the tracks or rust on the hardware. Old shutters may not meet current wind load codes.

**Your insurance company sent a notice.** If your carrier requires storm protection to renew, you have a deadline. Some policies drop homes in coastal zones without approved shutters.

**You have windows in the path of debris.** Ground floor windows and sliding glass doors are vulnerable. Protect these first if budget is tight.

**You live in a high wind corridor.** If your home sits directly on the coast or near the Intracoastal, wind speeds are higher. Upgrade to impact-rated shutters instead of standard aluminum.

## How to Hire a Shutter Contractor in Boca Raton

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Check at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified General Contractor (CGC) or Certified Roofing Contractor (CRC).

2. **Confirm active insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability and workers comp. Call the insurer to verify coverage is current.

3. **Check for Palm Beach County registration.** This confirms they are local, not a storm chaser from out of state. Look for a valid local business tax receipt.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates** with line-item breakdowns. Include material brand, motor type, warranty length, and permit responsibility.

5. **Check reviews on Google and BBB.** Look for patterns in complaints, not just star ratings. Pay attention to how they handle warranty issues.

6. **Never pay more than 10% upfront.** Florida Statute 489.126 restricts contractor deposits. Payment should tie to milestones: deposit, delivery, installation, final inspection.

7. **Get everything in writing.** Contract must include: scope, material specs with NOA numbers, dates, payment schedule, warranty terms, and cleanup plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install hurricane shutters in Boca Raton in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$8,000 and $25,000**. The average is around **$14,500**. Your cost depends on the number of windows, material type, and whether you choose manual or automatic systems.

### Do I need a permit to install shutters in Palm Beach County?

Yes, always. Apply through the Palm Beach County Building Department. Budget **$150–$400** for the fee. Two inspections required. Never let a contractor skip the permit — it can void your insurance and create title issues when you sell.

### How long does shutter installation take in Boca Raton?

Manual systems take **1–2 days** for an average home. Automatic systems take **2–4 days** due to wiring. Rainy season can add 2–5 days of weather delays.

### Does installing shutters increase home value in Boca Raton?

Shutters recoup **50–60%** of their cost at resale. The bigger value is insurability — a home with shutters is easier to sell because buyers know they are protected.

### Can I install hurricane shutters myself in Florida?

Florida law allows homeowners to do their own work if they own and occupy the home. But you still need permits and inspections. Insurance companies may refuse to cover a DIY installation. For most people, hiring a pro is safer.

### Does homeowners insurance cover shutter damage?

Wind and hail damage to shutters is typically covered under your property coverage. Normal wear and tear is never covered. Check your policy deductible — it is often **2% of insured value** for hurricane claims.
