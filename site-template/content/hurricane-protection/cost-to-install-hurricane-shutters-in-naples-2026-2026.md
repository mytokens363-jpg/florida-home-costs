# How Much Does It Cost to Install Hurricane Shutters in Naples? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing hurricane shutters in Naples costs between **$4,500 and $18,000** for a typical single-family home, with the average homeowner paying around **$9,800**. The final price depends heavily on the shutter style you choose, the number of windows and doors, and Collier County's strict building code requirements.

Naples sits right on the coast in Collier County, a zone where hurricane threats are real and frequent. The Florida Building Code and local ordinances demand that any shutters installed here meet specific impact and wind-load standards. This isn't just about aesthetics; it's about keeping your home standing when the wind picks up.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$4,500 – $7,200** | Roll-down or accordion, 10–12 openings |
| Mid-Range | **$7,200 – $12,500** | Colonial or Bahama, 15–20 openings |
| High-End | **$12,500 – $18,000+** | Motorized roll-down, 25+ openings |

**Budget** options usually involve manual roll-down shutters or basic accordion styles. These cover standard windows and perhaps one or two sliding glass doors. They are cost-effective but require manual operation or simple motorization.

**Mid-range** typically includes colonial shutters or Bahama shutters. These offer a classic Florida look while providing solid protection. They are common in Naples neighborhoods like Park Shore or Golden Gate Estates where curb appeal matters.

**High-end** covers fully motorized roll-down shutters or high-impact glass alternatives. These systems operate at the touch of a button and often include battery backup. They are ideal for larger homes with complex window layouts or frequent storms.

These prices reflect Naples market rates as of early 2026. They include materials, professional installation, necessary permits, and cleanup. They do not include structural reinforcement if your existing framing cannot support the shutter weight.

## What Affects the Cost in Naples

### 1. Shutter Style

The style you pick is the biggest driver of your total bill. Manual accordion shutters are the cheapest at **$15–$25 per sq ft** installed. Motorized roll-down units are the most expensive at **$35–$50 per sq ft** but offer the ultimate convenience. Colonial shutters fall in the middle at **$25–$35 per sq ft**, offering a permanent aesthetic.

### 2. Number of Openings

Naples homes often feature large sliding glass doors and picture windows to capture water views. Each opening adds a fixed cost for the track, housing, and installation time. A home with 10 standard windows and 2 doors might cost **$6,000**, while 25 openings could push the bill to **$15,000**.

### 3. Manual vs. Motorized

Manual systems require you to pull a crank or slide a track every time a storm approaches. Motorized systems connect to your home's electrical or a battery backup. The motor and wiring add **$1,500–$3,000** to a standard installation but save you physical labor during a panic.

### 4. Window and Door Size

Standard 36x80 inch doors are easy to price. However, Naples homes often have oversized sliders or custom arched windows. These require custom fabrication and heavier-duty tracks. Custom sizes can increase the per-square-foot cost by **20–30%** compared to standard sizes.

### 5. Collier County Permit Fees

Collier County requires a building permit for all permanent hurricane shutter installations. Permit fees are calculated based on the project value, typically ranging from **$150 to $450**. You must submit plans showing the wind-load rating and attachment details. The county does not issue permits for temporary plywood or drop-down units.

### 6. Structural Conditions

If your home was built before 2000, the framing around your windows might not be strong enough for heavy shutters like Bahama or Colonial styles. Reinforcing the header or adding blocking can add **$500–$1,500** to the project. Newer homes built to the 2002+ Florida Building Code usually need no reinforcement.

## Cost by Shutter Type

|--------------|---------------|----------|
| Accordion (Manual) | **$15 – $25** | 15–20 years |
| Accordion (Motorized) | **$25 – $35** | 15–20 years |
| Roll-Down (Manual) | **$20 – $30** | 20–25 years |
| Roll-Down (Motorized) | **$35 – $50** | 20–25 years |
| Colonial (Fixed) | **$25 – $35** | 25–30 years |

**Accordion shutters** are the most popular choice for their balance of price and protection. They fold away neatly to the side when not in use. Manual versions are affordable, while motorized versions add convenience for a higher upfront cost.

**Roll-down shutters** are the premium choice for security and ease. They retract into a compact box above the window. Motorized units are the standard here, offering remote control operation. They are excellent for homes with high ceilings or difficult-to-reach windows.

**Colonial shutters** are decorative but functional. They are hinged on the sides and can be tied back or locked shut. They provide a traditional Florida look that fits well in historic Naples districts. Installation is more labor-intensive than accordion styles.

**Bahama shutters** are hinged at the top and angle outward. They provide shade and ventilation when open while blocking wind when closed. They are heavier than colonial shutters and require robust mounting. They are a favorite for coastal homes seeking a tropical aesthetic.

**Plywood** is not a shutter but a common temporary measure. It costs **$2–$4 per sq ft** but looks ugly and offers no wind mitigation credit. It is not a permanent solution and does not meet the Florida Building Code for permanent protection.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) mandates that all shutters in Collier County meet specific wind-pressure and missile-impact tests. You must use products with a Miami-Dade NOA (Notice of Acceptance) or Florida Product Approval number. This ensures the shutter can withstand 170+ mph winds and 9-pound missile impacts.

**HVHZ Requirements:** While Naples is not in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) like Miami-Dade, it is in a high-risk coastal wind zone. The requirements are slightly less strict than the HVHZ but still demand rigorous testing. Products must be tested for uplift resistance and cyclic pressure loading.

**Permits:** Collier County uses the "ePlan" online portal for all building permits. You cannot just start installing; the permit must be approved first. Turnaround time is typically 3–7 business days for standard residential shutter projects. Fees range from **$150 to $450** based on the total project valuation.

**Insurance Impact:** Installing approved hurricane shutters can lower your wind insurance premium by **10–25%** in Florida. Most carriers require a "Wind Mitigation Inspection" to verify the installation. Without this inspection, you miss out on significant annual savings that can total thousands over the life of the policy.

**HOA Rules:** Naples has many strict Homeowners Associations, particularly in communities like Pelican Bay or Tiburon. Some HOAs ban roll-down shutters or specific colors. You must get architectural approval before purchasing. Installing the wrong style can lead to fines or forced removal.

**Season:** The best time to install is late spring to early fall, avoiding the peak of hurricane season (August–October). Contractors are busiest in July and August, so scheduling early ensures faster completion. Winter is ideal for installation but book months in advance due to high demand.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners with homesteaded properties built before 2008 can apply for grants up to **$10,000** through the My Safe Florida Home program. This program covers up to 50% of the cost for approved hurricane hardening measures, including shutter installation.

## How to Save Money on Shutter Installation in Naples

1. **Bundle all windows and doors.** Contractors charge a setup fee per job. Installing 5 sets is only slightly more expensive than 1 set if done together. Spread the fixed costs across more openings to lower the per-unit price by **15–20%**.

2. **Choose manual over motorized where possible.** If you are physically able to crank the shutters, skip the motor. The savings on a 10-opening job can be **$2,000–$3,500**. You can always add a motor later if your mobility changes.

3. **Get multiple quotes from local Naples contractors.** Prices vary significantly between companies. A quote from a Naples-based firm might be **$10,000** while a national chain charges **$14,000** for the same job. Get at least three detailed bids.

4. **Check for My Safe Florida Home grants.** If you qualify, this program can cut your out-of-pocket cost in half. The application process takes time, so start early before hurricane season hits.

5. **Time the installation for off-peak.** Contractors often have slower periods in late winter (January–February). Ask for a discount if you can schedule during this window. You might save **5–10%** on labor.

6. **Verify your HOA rules first.** Don't buy a style you can't use. Colonial shutters might be cheaper than roll-down in some HOAs because they don't require complex motor wiring.

7. **Consider financing options.** Many Naples shutter companies offer 0% APR financing for 12–24 months. This helps manage cash flow without paying high interest rates on credit cards.

## When to Install Shutters — Warning Signs

**You are 30 days from hurricane season.** It is too late to wait. Contractors are booked solid in July and August. You will face delays or exorbitant "rush" fees. Install in May or June to be safe.

**Your insurance carrier is requesting upgrades.** If your agent sends a letter stating your home is at risk due to lack of protection, act immediately. Some carriers will drop coverage if you don't upgrade within a specific timeframe.

**You live in a flood or wind zone.** Naples is a coastal city with frequent storm surges. Even if you don't live right on the water, high winds are common. Shutters protect against debris impact, which is the #1 cause of window failure.

**You have large sliding glass doors.** These are the weakest point in any home. A single broken pane can lead to rapid interior pressurization and roof failure. Prioritize these doors for the strongest shutter type.

**Your current shutters are damaged.** If you have old Bahama shutters that are rusted or accordion shutters with broken tracks, replace them now. Do not patch them. A failed shutter during a storm is worse than having none at all.

## How to Hire a Shutter Contractor in Naples

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Search for a Certified or Registered Contractor at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a license for General Contracting or specific Storm Shutters.

2. **Check for Collier County registration.** Ensure they have a local business tax receipt. This proves they are a legitimate local business, not a transient storm chaser.

3. **Ask for Miami-Dade NOA numbers.** Any reputable contractor should provide the NOA number for every shutter model they install. This is your proof of code compliance.

4. **Get a written warranty.** Look for a minimum 5-year warranty on materials and a 1-year warranty on labor. Some manufacturers offer 20-year warranties on the shutter mechanism itself.

5. **Check insurance coverage.** Ask for a certificate of insurance showing general liability and workers comp. If a worker falls off your ladder, you don't want to be liable.

6. **Read recent reviews.** Look for patterns in Google and Yelp reviews. Pay attention to comments about punctuality, cleanup, and post-installation support.

7. **Get a detailed contract.** The contract must list the exact model, color, finish, and square footage. It should also specify the permit responsibility and payment schedule.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install hurricane shutters in Naples in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$4,500 and $18,000**. The average is around **$9,800**. Your specific cost depends on the shutter type, number of openings, and whether you choose manual or motorized operation.

### Do I need a permit to install shutters in Collier County?

Yes, permanent shutter installations require a building permit. Apply through the Collier County ePlan portal. Fees range from **$150 to $450**. Temporary plywood or drop-down screens do not require permits.

### How long does a shutter installation take in Naples?

A standard 10-opening job takes **2–3 days**. Larger homes with 20+ openings can take **5–7 days**. Installation is usually faster than roofing but requires precise measurements and mounting.

### Do hurricane shutters lower my insurance premium?

Yes, approved shutters can reduce your wind premium by **10–25%**. You must have a Wind Mitigation Inspection to verify the installation and claim the discount.

### Can I install shutters myself in Florida?

Florida law allows homeowners to install shutters on their own primary residence. However, you still need permits and must meet all code requirements. Insurance companies may not honor the wind mitigation discount if you don't use a licensed installer.

### What is the best shutter type for Naples?

Accordion shutters are the most popular due to their cost and protection. Roll-down shutters offer the best convenience for motorized operation. Colonial and Bahama shutters are best for homes prioritizing curb appeal.

Installing hurricane shutters in Naples costs **$4,500 to $18,000** depending on your home's size and the style you choose.
