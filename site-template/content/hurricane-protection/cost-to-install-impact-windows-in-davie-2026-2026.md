# How Much Does It Cost to Install Impact Windows in Davie? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Installing impact-resistant windows in Davie costs between **$14,000 and $32,000** for a typical single-family home, with the average homeowner spending around **$21,500**. The final price depends heavily on the number of windows, frame material (aluminum vs. vinyl), and the strict Broward County permit requirements.

Davie sits right in the heart of Broward County, which falls under the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) rules for wind resistance. This means every single window you install must pass rigorous testing for missile impact and cyclic pressure. You cannot just buy standard "storm" windows; they must be certified to withstand flying debris at 150 mph or higher.

**Budget** projects usually involve replacing a standard set of 12 to 15 windows with entry-level vinyl frames. These units meet all Florida building codes but use standard double-pane glass without advanced energy-saving coatings.

**Mid-range** is the most common choice for Davie homeowners upgrading older homes. This includes 15 to 20 windows with durable aluminum frames, low-emissivity (low-e) glass to block heat, and upgraded locking mechanisms.

**High-end** covers large custom openings, sliding patio doors, and premium aluminum systems with thermal breaks. These installations often include multi-point locking systems and specialized glass tints for maximum UV protection.

These figures reflect Davie market rates for early 2026 and include the windows themselves, professional installation, disposal of old units, and the standard Broward County permit fees. They assume the existing rough openings are in decent condition.

## What Affects the Cost in Davie

### 1. Number of Windows

The most direct cost driver is simply how many units you need to replace. Davie homes typically range from 1,500 to 2,800 square feet, requiring between 12 and 20 windows. A full home replacement of 15 windows averages **$18,000**, while replacing just 5 windows might cost **$4,500–$6,000**.

### 2. Frame Material

Aluminum is the standard for South Florida due to its strength and resistance to salt air corrosion. Aluminum frames cost **$600–$900 per window** installed. Vinyl frames are cheaper at **$450–$650 per window** but can warp in extreme heat over time. Wood-clad options exist but are rare in this climate due to maintenance needs.

### 3. Glass Type and Features

Standard double-pane glass is the baseline. Upgrading to Low-E glass, which reflects heat and reduces UV damage, adds **$50–$100 per window**. Triple-pane glass is available but rarely recommended in Florida due to weight and cost. Laminated glass is required for impact ratings and adds **$150–$300 per window** compared to standard tempered glass.

### 4. Labor Rates in Broward County

Skilled window installers in Davie charge **$65–$95 per hour**. This is higher than the national average due to the specialized skills required for HVHZ installations. Complex installs, like those involving custom shapes or high ceilings, can push labor costs up by an additional **$1,000–$2,500** for the project.

### 5. Permit Costs

Broward County requires a building permit for all impact window replacements. The fee structure is based on the total project value, typically ranging from **$300 to $800** for a standard residential job. The process involves a plan review and a final inspection to ensure the installation meets the Florida Building Code.

### 6. Window Shape and Size

Standard rectangular windows are the most affordable. Custom shapes like circles, arches, or oversized sliding doors require custom manufacturing and special installation techniques. These custom units can cost **20–40% more** than standard sizes, with individual large doors reaching **$2,500–$4,000** each.

**Vinyl impact** windows are the budget-friendly option. They offer good thermal performance and meet all code requirements for Davie. While they look less premium than aluminum, they are perfectly functional and cost-effective for most homeowners.

**Aluminum impact** windows are the industry standard for Florida. They are incredibly strong, thin-profile, and resist the humid, salty air common in Davie. They cost more upfront but generally last longer and require less maintenance than vinyl.

**Wood-clad impact** windows combine the interior warmth of wood with an aluminum exterior shell. They are popular in high-end Davie estates but come with a steep price tag. They require more care to maintain the wood interior against humidity.

**Sliding doors** are essentially large impact windows. A standard 6-foot sliding glass door costs **$1,500–$2,200** installed. These are critical for hurricane protection as they represent the largest opening in a home's envelope.

**Custom arches** and specialty shapes require hand-crafting and custom framing. The cost jumps significantly because they cannot be mass-produced. These are usually found in Mediterranean or colonial-style homes in Davie's older neighborhoods.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** The Florida Building Code (7th/8th Edition) mandates that all windows in Davie must be tested to ASTM E1886 and E1996 standards. This involves shooting a 2x4 missile at the window at 34 mph and then pressurizing it to simulate hurricane winds. No window can be installed without a valid Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval.

**HVHZ Requirements:** Davie is located in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone. This zone covers all of Miami-Dade and Broward counties. Windows here must pass more rigorous testing than those in non-HVHZ areas of Florida. Your contractor must provide the specific NOA number for every product they install, or the permit will be rejected.

**Permits:** Broward County uses the "ePlan" online portal for permit submissions. You need to submit a plan showing the window locations, sizes, and NOA numbers. Permit fees are typically **$300–$800** depending on the total value. The county requires a final inspection before the installation is considered complete.

**Insurance Impact:** This is the biggest financial benefit. Installing impact windows can lower your windstorm insurance premium by **15–25%** in Broward County. Many insurers offer a "wind mitigation" discount specifically for impact-rated glazing. Some carriers may even require it for policy renewal on older homes.

**HOA Rules:** Davie has many active communities with strict architectural guidelines. Some HOAs restrict window colors, frame styles, or require aluminum over vinyl for aesthetic consistency. Always get written HOA approval before ordering windows to avoid being forced to remove non-compliant units.

**Season:** The best time to install windows in Davie is during the dry season, from November to April. Installing during the rainy season (May to October) risks water damage to your interior if the old windows are removed before the new ones are sealed. Rain delays can add **1–3 days** to the project timeline.

**My Safe Florida Home:** Homeowners with older homes may qualify for the My Safe Florida Home program. This offers a free inspection and matching grants up to **$10,000** for impact window installation if the home is homesteaded and built before 2008. Check the state website for current eligibility.

## How to Save Money on Impact Window Installation in Davie

1. **Bundle the project.** Replacing all windows at once is significantly cheaper than doing them in stages. Contractors often offer a **10–15% discount** on the total labor cost when the entire home is done in one go. It also reduces the number of permits needed.

2. **Stick to standard sizes.** Custom shapes and non-standard dimensions drive up costs. If you are willing to keep your existing window openings, you can save **$200–$500 per window**. Only order custom sizes if you are changing the window layout entirely.

3. **Choose aluminum over vinyl for durability.** While vinyl is cheaper upfront, aluminum lasts longer in Davie's climate. The long-term value is better, and aluminum frames often come with better warranties. The **$150–$250** price difference per window is often worth it for the lifespan.

4. **Check for insurance discounts first.** Call your insurance provider before signing a contract. If they offer a specific discount for impact windows, factor that into your ROI calculation. Some insurers provide a list of approved installers that might offer better pricing.

5. **Get 3–4 detailed quotes.** Pricing varies by **20–30%** between contractors in Davie. Ensure each quote includes the same NOA numbers, glass type, and warranty terms. Do not just compare the bottom line; look at the line items to see what is included.

## When to Install Impact Windows — Warning Signs

**Your current windows are older than 20 years.** Most pre-2000s windows in Davie were not built to current impact standards. They may not hold up against a direct hit from hurricane debris. If they are failing to keep out heat or drafts, it is time to upgrade.

**You live in a high-wind zone.** Davie is prone to tropical storms and hurricanes. If your home is in a designated flood zone or coastal area, standard windows are a liability. Impact windows are the only way to guarantee your home won't breach during a storm.

**Your insurance premiums are skyrocketing.** If your windstorm premium is rising or your insurer is asking for a roof or window inspection, impact windows can be the solution. Installing them can stabilize your premiums and prevent non-renewal.

**You notice water or air leaks.** If you feel drafts or see water intrusion during heavy rain, your windows have likely failed. In Davie's humidity, this leads to mold and structural rot. Impact windows provide a superior seal that stops air and water infiltration.

## How to Hire a Window Contractor in Davie

1. **Verify their Florida license.** Use [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com) to check for a Certified or Registered contractor. Look for "CGC" (General Contractor) or "CAC" (Air Conditioning) licenses that include glazing work.

2. **Ask for NOA numbers.** Before signing anything, ask the contractor for the Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) number for the specific window brand they plan to use. If they hesitate or don't have it, do not hire them.

3. **Check for local business tax receipts.** Davie requires a local business tax receipt (formerly occupational license). This confirms the contractor is registered with the City of Davie, not just the state.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates.** Each quote should list the brand, model, NOA number, glass type, frame color, and total cost. It should also specify who pulls the permit and handles the final inspection.

5. **Check reviews and references.** Look for reviews on Google and the BBB specifically mentioning "impact windows" and "Davie." Ask for references from recent jobs in Broward County.

6. **Never pay 100% upfront.** Florida law limits deposits to **10%** of the total contract price. Payments should be tied to milestones: deposit, delivery of windows, installation, and final inspection approval.

7. **Ensure warranty coverage.** Impact windows come with a manufacturer's warranty (often 10–20 years) and a workmanship warranty from the installer. Make sure the workmanship warranty is at least **2 years** and is in writing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install impact windows in Davie in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$14,000 and $32,000** for a full home replacement. The average is around **$21,500**. Your specific cost depends on the number of windows, frame material, and whether you need custom sizes.

### Do I need a permit to install impact windows in Davie?

Yes, absolutely. Broward County requires a building permit for all window replacements. The process involves submitting plans with NOA numbers and paying a fee of **$300–$800**. The county will inspect the installation to ensure it meets code.

### How long does the installation take?

A full home installation of 15–20 windows typically takes **2–4 days** for a professional crew. Custom orders may take **4–6 weeks** to arrive from the manufacturer before installation can begin.

### Will impact windows lower my insurance premium?

Yes. Installing impact windows in Davie can lower your windstorm insurance premium by **15–25%**. This is a significant savings that can offset the installation cost over time.

### Can I install impact windows myself in Florida?

Florida law allows homeowners to install windows on their own primary residence if they pull the permit. However, the installation must still meet HVHZ code standards. Insurance companies may deny claims if the installation is not done by a licensed professional.

### What is the difference between impact windows and storm shutters?

Impact windows are permanently installed glass that resists debris. Storm shutters are removable covers. Impact windows are more convenient and often provide better security and energy efficiency, though they cost more upfront.
