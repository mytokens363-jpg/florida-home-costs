# How Much Does It Cost to Replace a Flat Roof in Davie? (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** A full flat roof replacement in Davie costs between **$8,000 and $20,000** for most residential homes, with the average homeowner paying around **$12,500**. The biggest factors driving your price are roof square footage, material choice, and Broward County's strict building permit requirements.

Flat roofs are common on mid-century ranches and newer additions in Davie. They require different materials and installation methods than standard pitched roofs. That changes the pricing structure significantly.

## Average Cost Breakdown

| Cost Level | Price Range | Typical Scope |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Budget | **$8,000 – $11,000** | 2,000 sq ft, TPO single ply |
| Mid-Range | **$11,000 – $15,500** | 2,500 sq ft, Modified Bitumen |
| High-End | **$15,500 – $20,000+** | PVC, 4,000 sq ft, complex drains |

**Budget** gets you standard TPO (thermoplastic polyolefin) on a simple, single-story structure with basic tear-off and disposal. It meets code but uses standard thickness materials.

**Mid-range** is what most Davie homeowners choose — modified bitumen or thicker TPO membranes with enhanced flashing and drainage improvements. Covers homes in the 2,000 to 3,000 sq ft range.

**High-end** covers reinforced PVC or multi-layer systems on larger homes or complex geometries. Includes upgraded insulation, new drains, and enhanced perimeter flashing systems.

These prices reflect Davie and Broward County market rates as of early 2026 and include materials, labor, tear-off and disposal, standard permits, and cleanup. They do not include structural repairs if damage is found during tear-off.

## What Affects the Cost in Davie

### 1. Roof Size

Flat roofing is priced per "square" (100 square feet). A typical Davie ranch home has a flat roof section between 1,500 and 3,000 sq ft. At **$4.00–$8.00 per square foot** installed depending on material, a 1,500 sq ft roof runs roughly **$8,000–$12,000** while a 3,000 sq ft roof pushes **$15,000–$20,000**.

### 2. Material Choice

This is the single biggest price lever you control. TPO membranes are the most common option at **$4.00–$6.00 per sq ft** installed. Modified bitumen — popular for re-roofs — jumps to **$5.50–$8.00**. PVC is the premium choice at **$7.00–$10.00 per sq ft** but resists chemicals and heat better.

### 3. Labor Rates in Broward County

Roofing labor in Broward County runs **$50–$75 per hour**, roughly 15–20% above the national average. Demand stays high year-round from storm damage repairs and insurance-mandated replacements. After a named storm, rates can spike another 10–20%.

### 4. Permits and Inspections

Broward County requires a building permit for any roof replacement. Permit fees run **$300–$750** depending on project value. The process includes a pre-inspection and final inspection. Your contractor should pull the permit — if they suggest skipping it, walk away.

### 5. Roof Complexity

A simple single-layer flat roof is straightforward to replace. Add drains, vents, skylights, or multiple levels and labor climbs. Complex roofs can add **$2,500–$6,000** to the project. Drainage issues are common in flat roofs and require more planning.

### 6. FBC Wind Requirements

Davie falls under Florida Building Code Zone 2 or 3 wind requirements. Every roofing product must be approved for these wind loads. This limits material options and adds **$1,000–$2,500** compared to non-wind zones. Fastener schedules are stricter than standard code.

## Cost by Material Type

| Material | Cost per Sq Ft | Lifespan |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| TPO | **$4.00 – $6.00** | 15–20 years |
| EPDM | **$5.00 – $7.50** | 20–30 years |
| Modified Bitumen | **$5.50 – $8.00** | 15–20 years |
| PVC | **$7.00 – $10.00** | 20–30 years |
| Built-Up Roof | **$8.00 – $12.00** | 20–30 years |
| Green Roof | **$15.00+** | 30+ years |

**TPO** is the budget option. Reflective white surface helps with cooling bills. Best for standard residential applications in Davie.

**EPDM** (rubber) hits the sweet spot — resistant to UV, 20-year lifespan, and the best cost-to-value ratio. This is what many homeowners pick for older ranches.

**Modified bitumen** is the classic flat roof material. Torch-down application is common. Rated for moderate wind but requires skilled labor to torch properly.

**PVC** is premium — 30+ year lifespan, heat reflective, and resistant to chemical damage. Expect to pay **$7.00–$10.00 per sq ft** for this system.

**Built-up roofs** offer maximum durability at 20–30 years with multiple layers of asphalt and gravel. Best for homeowners who want a "last roof you'll ever buy" solution. Higher upfront cost pays off over decades.

**Green roofs** are specific to eco-conscious homeowners. Different product category entirely from standard roofing materials.

## Florida-Specific Considerations

**Building Codes:** Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023) governs all roof replacements in Davie. Key requirements include specific drainage slope calculations, proper flashing at all penetrations, and wind uplift resistance. These go beyond what most states require for flat roofs.

**Wind Zone:** Davie is not in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone like Miami, but it is in a Windstorm Zone. All roofing materials must hold a Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA for wind resistance. Your contractor should provide approval numbers for every product they plan to use.

**Permits:** Apply through Broward County's ePlan online portal. Turnaround is 5–10 business days for a standard residential permit. Budget **$300–$750** for the fee. Two inspections required: pre-cover and final.

**Insurance Impact:** This is the big one. A new roof resets the clock with your insurance company. Many Florida insurers won't write or renew policies on homes with roofs older than 15 years. Upgrading to a hip roof shape or adding hurricane straps during replacement can qualify you for wind mitigation discounts of **15–45%** on your premium.

**HOA:** Many Davie communities restrict roofing material, color, and style. Tile is often required in deed-restricted communities. Get written HOA approval before signing a contractor agreement — changing materials mid-job is extremely expensive.

**Season:** Best time to replace a roof here is December through April — dry season, lower humidity, fewer storm damage backlogs. Avoid peak hurricane season (August–October). Rainy season (May–October) brings daily afternoon thunderstorms that delay projects.

**My Safe Florida Home:** If your home was built before 2008 and is homesteaded, you may qualify for a free wind inspection and matching grants up to **$10,000** toward hurricane hardening improvements including roof upgrades.

## How to Save Money on Roof Replacement in Davie

1. **Get at least 4 written quotes.** Contractor pricing varies by 20–35% for identical scope in Davie. On a **$12,000** job, that's **$2,500–$4,000** in potential savings. Make sure each quote itemizes materials, labor, tear-off, permits, and disposal separately.

2. **Schedule in dry season.** December through April is slower for roofers. You'll have more leverage to negotiate and fewer weather delays. Some contractors offer **5–10%** off-season discounts.

3. **Choose TPO or EPDM if your HOA allows it.** The savings between TPO (**$4.00–$6.00/sq ft**) and PVC (**$7.00–$10.00/sq ft**) can be **$3,000–$6,000** on a typical home.

4. **Bundle drainage upgrades with the roof.** Adding new drains or upgrading scuppers during a replacement is **40–60% cheaper** than standalone projects. The combined improvements can also cut your insurance premium by hundreds per year.

5. **Check for insurance claim eligibility.** If your existing roof has storm damage, your insurance may cover part or all of the replacement. File the claim before signing a contractor agreement. Be cautious of contractors who offer to "handle the insurance" — work with your adjuster directly.

## When to Replace Your Roof — Warning Signs

**Your roof is 15–20 years old.** Flat roofs in South Florida degrade faster than in cooler climates due to intense UV, heat cycling, and rain. A 20-year-old flat roof in Davie has taken more abuse than a 30-year-old roof in the Midwest.

**Your insurance company is pressuring you.** A non-renewal notice or letter requesting a roof inspection means the clock is ticking. Many Florida insurers are actively dropping homes with aging roofs.

**Visible pooling water on the surface.** Standing water that doesn't drain after 48 hours indicates slope issues or clogged drains. This accelerates membrane breakdown and leads to leaks.

**Interior water stains or attic moisture.** Brown ceiling spots, bubbling paint, or damp insulation point to roof leaks. In Davie's humidity, even small leaks lead to mold fast. Multiple leaks usually mean the whole system has failed.

## How to Hire a Roofing Contractor in Davie

1. **Verify their Florida license** at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com). You need a Certified Roofing Contractor (CCC), Registered Roofing Contractor (RC), or General Contractor (CGC) license.

2. **Confirm active insurance.** Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing general liability (minimum $1 million) and workers comp. Call the insurer directly to verify.

3. **Check for a Broward County local business tax receipt.** This confirms they're registered locally, not a storm chaser from out of state.

4. **Get 3–4 written estimates** with line-item breakdowns — material brand, membrane thickness, number of squares, tear-off, permits, labor, timeline, and warranty.

5. **Check reviews on Google and BBB.** Look for patterns in complaints, not just star ratings.

6. **Never pay more than 10% upfront.** Florida Statute 489.126 restricts contractor deposits. Payment should tie to milestones: deposit, tear-off complete, mid-project, final inspection passed.

7. **Get everything in writing.** Contract must include: scope, material specs with approval numbers, dates, payment schedule, warranty terms, permit responsibility, and cleanup plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a flat roof replacement cost in Davie in 2026?

Most homeowners pay between **$8,000 and $20,000**. The average is around **$12,500**. Your actual cost depends on roof size, material choice, complexity, and whether structural repairs are needed.

### Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Broward County?

Yes, always. Apply through Broward County's ePlan portal. Budget **$300–$750**. Two inspections required. Never let a contractor skip the permit — it can void your insurance and create title issues when you sell.

### How long does a flat roof replacement take in Davie?

Standard TPO roofs take **2–4 days** for an average home. Modified bitumen takes **4–7 days**. Rainy season can add 2–5 days of weather delays.

### Does a new roof increase home value in Davie?

A new roof recoups **60–70%** of its cost at resale. But the bigger value in Davie's market is insurability — a home with a new roof is dramatically easier to sell because buyers can actually get insurance on it.

### Can I replace my own roof in Florida?

Florida's homeowner exemption allows it if you own and occupy the home. But you still need permits, inspections, and must meet all FBC code requirements. Insurance companies may refuse to cover a homeowner-installed roof. For most people, it's not worth the risk.

### Does homeowners insurance cover roof replacement?

Storm damage (wind, hail, fallen trees) is typically covered minus your deductible — often **2% of insured value** for hurricane claims in Florida. Normal wear and aging is never covered.
